RISE DTC

Video scripts, batch one

5 videosOne recording session60 to 90 seconds each

Five talking-head scripts, built from your own sales calls and your real client numbers. Record them back to back in one sitting, we release one per week. Every script opens on the claim in the first three seconds and ends on a question for the comments.

The one-sitting setup: phone on a tripod at eye level, 4 to 8 feet back, shoot vertical. Face a window, overheads off. Mic 15 to 25 cm away, test on headphones before the first real take: bad audio is the only thing that reads as cheap. Then per video:
  • Record the hook 2 or 3 ways, then the body, then one safety take.
  • Keep a notebook or iPad on the desk and write the numbers as you say them. Same desk every video.
  • Raw beats polished here. One relaxed pass, fumbles left in, reads human and performs better.
Publishing, every video: post 4:5 vertical with captions on, most people watch muted. No links in the post or comments. Say the ask out loud on camera. Reply to comments in the first hour, that window decides the reach. One video per week, Tuesday to Thursday.
