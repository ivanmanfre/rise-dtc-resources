Why this matters now

The comparison happens before anyone reaches your website

Written by Mattan Danino, CEO of RISE DTC · 31 July 2026 · No email, no form, nothing gated.

A study published on 14 July 2026 watched 56 people compare products inside ChatGPT across 221 codable shopping tasks. 92.8% of those tasks ended with no meaningful click out to the open web. Whatever the chat window put on the screen was the whole set of options those shoppers weighed.

A second study from the same vendor sampled 201,137 ChatGPT shopping prompt runs containing 812,190 product cards over one week in June 2026. 707,752 of those cards, 87.3%, were retrieved from the web crawl. 102,523 of them, 12.7%, came from direct merchant product feeds.

Both numbers are secondary sources, both are one vendor's sample, and both are named and dated below so you can weigh them yourself. The 92.8% is a screened panel of 56 people. The 87.3% is one week. Read the exact figures as directional and the direction as real.

Here is where the three platforms actually sit as of early August 2026. OpenAI gates product-feed ingestion behind an application. Google gates direct buying on AI Mode and Gemini behind a UCP waitlist and an approval, on top of your existing Merchant Center feed. Anthropic's crawler documentation contains no merchant program at all. The one lever all three of them document, and the only one you control today without anyone's approval, is your own site: robots.txt access for their bots, and a live product page good enough to be the answer they fetch. Crawl here means both the scheduled crawl and the fetch a chat product makes at question time.

Everything below traces to a document one of those four companies published. Where a page is silent on something, this guide prints the silence instead of filling it in.

01

Platform 01 · OpenAI

Two crawlers, one gated feed, and an ad slot beside the answer

OpenAI runs two robots.txt tokens that matter to a store, and they do different jobs. OAI-SearchBot is the search crawler. GPTBot is the training crawler. OpenAI's crawler documentation states that each setting is independent of the others, so allowing one commits you to nothing about the other. The same page notes it can take around 24 hours from a robots.txt change for OpenAI's systems to adjust.

The block-side wording is the part worth reading twice, and it is quoted below. Open yourstore.com/robots.txt and look for OAI-SearchBot. No rule at all means allowed, which is where most Shopify stores sit. The ones that get burned are stores where an SEO app or a hand-edited robots.txt.liquid added a block nobody remembers writing. The checker further down this page reads a pasted file and tells you which of the two you have.

The direct product feed is a real program and it is gated. As of early August 2026, OpenAI's commerce documentation says onboarding product feeds is currently available to approved partners, and the application is a form at chatgpt.com/merchants. There is no self serve switch behind it. The specification is public, so you can size the engineering before you apply: feeds go over SFTP as a full catalog snapshot, with a recommended cadence of at least daily.

One scoping note before anyone quotes this page back at me. ChatGPT carries advertising. OpenAI began testing ads in ChatGPT on 9 February 2026, in the United States, on the Free and Go tiers, and OpenAI states that ads do not influence the answers ChatGPT gives you. Everything in this guide is about the organic product unit, which is the one your pages and your data can move.

OAI-SearchBot, the block side

"Sites that are opted out of OAI-SearchBot will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers, though can still appear as navigational links."

OpenAI, Overview of OpenAI Crawlers · read 31 July 2026 · developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots
The two settings are independent

"Each setting is independent of the others – for example, a webmaster can allow OAI-SearchBot in order to appear in search results while disallowing GPTBot to indicate that crawled content should not be used for training OpenAI's generative AI foundation models."

OpenAI, Overview of OpenAI Crawlers · the same page states search results can take around 24 hours to reflect a robots.txt update · developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots
The feed is an application, as of early August 2026

"Onboarding product feeds in ChatGPT is currently available to approved partners. To apply for access, fill out this form here"

OpenAI commerce documentation, Get Started · the form links to chatgpt.com/merchants · developers.openai.com
The ad unit, scoped

"Ads do not influence the answers ChatGPT gives you, and we keep your conversations with ChatGPT private from advertisers."

OpenAI, Testing ads in ChatGPT · originally published 9 February 2026, a test for logged in adult users on the Free and Go subscription tiers in the United States · openai.com
02

Platform 02 · Google

UCP is a buying door, and Google-Extended is the switch people get wrong

Google's merchant program here is the Universal Commerce Protocol. Google describes UCP as an open standard for turning AI interactions into instant sales, and says adopting it enables agentic actions on AI Mode in Google Search and Gemini, starting with direct buying. The surfaces named are AI Mode in Google Search and Gemini web, with the app listed as coming soon.

You do not build a new feed for it. Google says merchants use their existing Merchant Center account shopping feeds. What you do need is to be let in. As of early August 2026 the way in is a waitlist, and Google's UCP guides page states that your integration must be approved by Google before you can go live on AI Mode in Google Search and Gemini. Embedded checkout is described as available to specific, approved merchants. So UCP is a direct-buy capability with a queue in front of it, and it is worth applying for, not worth waiting on.

Now the switch people get wrong. Google-Extended is a robots.txt token, and Google's crawler documentation defines exactly what it governs: whether content Google crawls from your site may be used for training future generations of Gemini models, and for grounding, which the same page defines as providing content to the model at prompt time to improve factuality and relevancy. Those two things travel together in Google's own sentence. The same page states that Google-Extended does not impact a site's inclusion in Google Search nor is it used as a ranking signal in Google Search.

That page does not name AI Overviews or AI Mode anywhere in connection with Google-Extended. So this guide will not tell you what blocking it does or does not do to those surfaces, in either direction. The documentation does not say, and inventing the answer is how a founder ends up making an expensive change on a sentence nobody wrote. Googlebot is a separate token, and Google states that preferences addressed to Googlebot affect Google Search including Discover and all Search features, as well as Google Images, Video and News. Leave Googlebot alone.

What UCP is for

"Adopt UCP to enable agentic actions on AI Mode in Google Search and Gemini, starting with direct buying."

Google, Universal Commerce Protocol · the same page names the surfaces as "AI Mode in Google Search and Gemini web (app coming soon)" and says merchants "use your existing Merchant Center account shopping feeds" · read 31 July 2026 · developers.google.com/merchant/ucp
The approval, as of early August 2026

"Your integration must be approved by Google before you can go live on AI Mode in Google Search and Gemini."

Google, UCP guides · this sentence is on the guides page, not the UCP overview · developers.google.com/merchant/ucp/guides
What Google-Extended governs

"Google-Extended is a standalone product token that web publishers can use to manage whether content Google crawls from their sites may be used for training future generations of Gemini models that power Gemini Apps and Vertex AI API for Gemini and for grounding (providing content from the Google Search index to the model at prompt time to improve factuality and relevancy) in Gemini Apps and Grounding with Google Search on Vertex AI."

Google Search Central, Google common crawlers · the same page states "Google-Extended does not impact a site's inclusion in Google Search nor is it used as a ranking signal in Google Search" · read 31 July 2026 · developers.google.com
Googlebot, for contrast

"Crawling preferences addressed to the Googlebot user agent affect Google Search (including Discover and all Google Search features), as well as other products such as Google Images, Google Video, Google News, and Discover."

Google Search Central, Google common crawlers · developers.google.com
03

Platform 03 · Anthropic

Three crawlers, and a merchant program the documentation does not contain

Anthropic publishes one page for site owners, and it documents three user agents. ClaudeBot collects web content that could potentially contribute to training. Claude-User is the agent that may access websites when individuals ask Claude questions. Claude-SearchBot navigates the web to improve search result quality. All three are quoted below in Anthropic's own words.

Read Claude-User again, because it is the one operators miss. Claude already reads and cites live pages at the moment a person asks a question. If your robots.txt blocks Claude-User, that is the fetch you are turning down.

Here is the part that makes this platform different, and here is exactly how far I will take it. That documentation contains no product feed, no merchant program, no shopping surface and no product carousel for retailers. I re-grepped it on 31 July 2026 for merchant, feed, shopping, product, carousel and retail, and the article text returns nothing commerce related. That is a statement about what the documentation contains as of early August 2026, and it is not a statement about what Anthropic is planning or partnering on. Do not let anyone stretch it into one.

What it means for a store this week is simple. There is nothing to apply to. The only thing you can do for Claude is the same thing that pays on the other two: let the crawlers in, and have a product page worth quoting.

ClaudeBot

"helps enhance the utility and safety of our generative AI models by collecting web content that could potentially contribute to their training."

Anthropic support, Does Anthropic crawl data from the web · read 31 July 2026 · support.claude.com
Claude-User

"supports Claude AI users. When individuals ask questions to Claude, it may access websites using a Claude-User agent."

Anthropic support, same page · support.claude.com
Claude-SearchBot

"navigates the web to improve search result quality for users. It analyzes online content specifically to enhance the relevance and accuracy of search responses."

Anthropic support, same page · the page also publishes the robots.txt forms for these agents · support.claude.com
04

The console · Shopify

One admin section decides which AI channels can sell your products

If you are on Shopify, there is a single place in your admin that governs this. Shopify's help documentation says agentic storefronts let customers discover and purchase your products in AI channels, that your products are made available to those channels through Shopify Catalog, and that you manage which AI channels you want to sell your products on under Sales channels then Agentic.

The live help doc names ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, and Meta, the last one through the Facebook and Instagram by Meta sales channel. Treat that as what Shopify names today rather than a closed list. It grew by one channel in the week this guide was written, which is the whole reason I am telling you to open the page and read it rather than trust a screenshot.

Shopify says agentic storefronts is active by default for eligible stores. Eligible is doing real work in that sentence. Open the toggle, read what is switched on for your store, and write down what you find. Do not assume you are in, and do not assume you are out.

This console is about selling and purchasing through Shopify Catalog. It is a different mechanism from the robots.txt switches in the next section, and turning one on does nothing to the other.

What the console does

"Shopify agentic storefronts let customers discover and purchase your products in AI channels, such as ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot."

Shopify Help Center, Shopify agentic storefronts · the live page family also names Meta, through "the Facebook and Instagram by Meta sales channel", re-read 31 July 2026 · help.shopify.com
Default on, for eligible stores

"Agentic storefronts is active by default for eligible stores."

Shopify Help Center, agentic storefronts setup · the same page family describes managing channels in "Sales channels>Agentic in your Shopify admin" · help.shopify.com

The file · robots.txt

Seven switches, one file, and nobody has to approve it

Everything above needs somebody's permission except this. OpenAI documents OAI-SearchBot and GPTBot. Google documents Google-Extended and Googlebot. Anthropic documents ClaudeBot, Claude-User and Claude-SearchBot. That is seven tokens, and all seven are read from one file at yourstore.com/robots.txt.

Paste your file into the checker below and it will tell you, token by token, whether the root path is blocked or allowed and what each state means according to the company that defined that token. It runs entirely in this page. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored.

One footnote that belongs here rather than in the tool. OpenAI documents an eighth agent, ChatGPT-User, for user initiated fetches, and states that because those actions are initiated by a user, robots.txt rules may not apply. The same page points site owners at OAI-SearchBot for managing search opt outs. So ChatGPT-User is worth knowing about and it is not a robots.txt lever, which is why the checker does not print a verdict for it.

Two rules I will state flatly. Never block Googlebot. And a change here takes time to land, so read your file, fix it, then look again tomorrow.

The ChatGPT-User footnote

"ChatGPT-User is not used for crawling the web in an automatic fashion. Because these actions are initiated by a user, robots.txt rules may not apply. ChatGPT-User is not used to determine whether content may appear in Search. Please use OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt for managing Search opt outs and automatic crawl."

OpenAI, Overview of OpenAI Crawlers · read 31 July 2026 · developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots
Robots.txt checker

Open yourstore.com/robots.txt in a browser, select all, and paste the whole thing here. The checker parses the User-agent groups and the Allow and Disallow rules, then reports the verdict for the root path for each of the seven tokens plus the catch-all group.

Load a sample file

Paste only. This runs in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere, nothing is saved, and there is no email field on this page. The verdict is for the root path only, which is the one that decides whether a crawler can read your store at all. Path level rules on specific URLs are a separate question and this tool does not answer it.

The lines most stores end up wanting

This is the combination most DTC brands land on once they have read the four documents above: search and question-time fetching allowed everywhere, model training kept out. Every one of these settings is independent of the others, so take the half you want and leave the rest.

Google-Extended is deliberately not in that block. It is a training and grounding switch, and which way you set it is a judgment call about Gemini, not a fix. Googlebot is not in it either, and it should stay out of your robots.txt as a block under any circumstances.

Do this week

Four jobs, in the order they pay

The first two cost you an afternoon and need nobody's approval. The third is a five minute check most founders assume they have already done. The fourth is a queue, so join it and get back to work.

01 · Read your robots.txt

Open yourstore.com/robots.txt, paste the whole file into the checker above, and look at the seven verdicts. If a crawler you want is blocked, the fix is a few lines and it is yours to make. Check again tomorrow, because OpenAI's own doc says search results can take around 24 hours to reflect the change.

02 · Make the product page the answer

87.3% of product cards in that June sample were retrieved from the web crawl. Specs, sizing, materials, care, dimensions, and a straight answer to the question your buyer types. That page is what gets fetched, quoted and compared, on all three platforms.

03 · Open Sales channels then Agentic

In your Shopify admin. Read which AI channels are switched on for your store and write it down. Shopify says it is active by default for eligible stores, and eligible is the word that decides whether that sentence is about you.

04 · Join the two queues

Apply for the OpenAI product feed at chatgpt.com/merchants and join the Google UCP waitlist. Both are gated as of early August 2026, so neither is a thing you can finish this week. Jobs 01 to 03 are, and they cover the crawl either way.

Every source on this page was read at its live URL on 31 July 2026 and is linked in full. Two of the citations are secondary, both from Profound, and both are named and dated where they appear so you can weigh them accordingly. Where a document is silent, this page prints the silence. Program status can change quickly: the Shopify channel list gained a channel in the week this was written, so re-read the pages before you act on a program claim.
