About the research in this playbook

What the report measured.

Graphite and Similarweb tracked ad reach, clicks, creative and landing pages. Here are the findings that affect what you write, which page you use and what you can afford to spend.

What was measured

Audience reach, ad clicks, advertiser activity, creative and landing pages. The report combines a user panel with separate ad and page datasets. Its PDF methodology lists 95,000+ ads from 500+ US advertisers, 70,000+ distinct creatives, 50,000+ headlines and 8,000+ classified landing pages.

What your store still needs

Your order economics, conversion measurement and a budget you can commit to a test. The report provides no DTC customer acquisition cost or profit benchmark.

Each chart covers the period and sample shown beside it. “Top” usually means the advertisers with the largest share of impressions. Sales and profit are outside that ranking.

06 Your ad and budget

Choose your ad, page and spending limit.

Across ten US advertisers, only 1.9% of ChatGPT headlines matched Google Search exactly. In the 400+ US advertiser landing-page sample, 60% used five pages or fewer. Neither finding tells you what a customer will cost. Start with one product and the amount you can afford to pay for its first order.

  1. Choose the offer. Write the buyer’s situation and the product fact that makes the offer relevant.
  2. Calculate the acquisition ceiling. Start from net sales and subtract variable order costs. Reserve the amount you need to retain after acquisition.
  3. Connect the ad to its page. Save the headline, offer and page URL together. Check the product details are visible on a phone.
  4. Set the test budget. Include separate setup and production costs. Write down the spending cap and who will check it.
  5. Decide when to check the orders. Allow time for delayed orders and returns. Set the cost limit for continuing, and the problems that would make you pause or change the ad. You may need more orders before deciding.

An attributed order count does not establish how many customers were caused by the ads. Keep that question separate when deciding whether to expand the test.
