The setup, step by step

Four stops from install to live

OpenAI's own order, with the skill to run before each stop. OpenAI's setup article

Screenshot: OpenAI

1Connect

Install the app and connect your account

  • Install from Chrome or Safari.
  • Select Connect Account.
  • Pick your Ads Manager account, or create one.
  • Have ready: website, industry, country, currency, time zone.
Run first: chatgpt-ads-readiness-check

Catches a blocked robots.txt, the wrong browser and an ineligible category before you start.

Watch out. "Unable to verify this connection attempt" is a known error on the listing. Check the browser and the account, then write to ads-support@openai.com.

Screenshot: OpenAI

2Sync

Connect the pixel and let the catalog sync

  • Select or create the OpenAI Pixel for Shopify.
  • Connect it so purchases reach Ads Manager.
  • Wait for the product sync. Bigger catalogs take longer.
Run first: catalog-rewriter-for-chatgpt

The feed copies your product rows as they are at sync. Fix the thin ones first.

Watch out. The Products tab stays empty until a campaign has delivered. That is normal.

Screenshot: OpenAI

3Launch

Build the first campaign inside Shopify

  • Create Campaign: name, budget, optimization event, targeting.
  • Ad type: all products, single product or website.
  • Pick the products, check the preview, launch.
Run first: chatgpt-ads-click-math

Tells you the conversion rate your bid needs before money moves.

Watch out. The objective, the billing model and the conversion event cannot be changed later. A different choice means a new campaign.

Screenshot: OpenAI

4Budget

Know what a daily budget can bill

  • Minimum $25 a day in USD.
  • One day can bill up to 2x your daily budget.
  • A Sunday to Saturday week bills at most 7x.
  • In Shopify budgets move in $25 steps. Ads Manager takes any amount.
Run first: chatgpt-ads-click-math

Prints both ceilings from your own budget.

Watch out. Give it 24 hours before calling zero impressions a fault. Reporting lags delivery by up to 7 hours.

How to install

Three steps, any ChatGPT plan

  • Download the kit and unzip it.
  • Open a skill folder, copy its SKILL.md, paste it into a new chat.
  • Paste your own details underneath and send.
›Download all 5 skills
On ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Edu: install as real Skills
  1. In ChatGPT, open the sidebar, go to Plugins, then Skills, then Create.
  2. Choose Upload from your computer and upload one skill folder at a time.
  3. Type @chatgpt-ads-readiness-check in a chat and paste your details.

OpenAI's help center says the Skills upload is for Business, Enterprise, Healthcare and Edu seats. Free, Plus and Pro use the three steps above.

Worth knowing

What these will not do

  • Connect to anything

    You paste, they read. No key, no permission.

  • Promise approval

    OpenAI reviews every advertiser and every ad.

  • Quote a benchmark

    OpenAI publishes none, so the kit carries none.

  • Guess

    A missing input prints [NEEDS:] with the exact field.
