01 / Paid ads

Test Meta ads for the Green Sage racket

Your two Google text ads announce the Green racket’s return; Meta’s ad library currently shows no ads on your Facebook page. With Green the only racket available in a limited run, stock sets a ceiling on how much paid demand the store can fulfil.

One changeProposed UK Meta test: compare two ads using the same Green racket photo and product page, one leading with “Green Sage is back, in a limited run only.” and the other with “Now with a sandy surface to help grip the ball for those spin shots!” Exclude prior buyers and recent site visitors, and cap spending to the batch’s stock and margin.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
Give each ad equal budget. Compare cost per first-time purchase and margin after ads; small samples remain inconclusive, and both ads pause when stock runs low.

03 / Your store

Offer a £25 cap beside the Green racket button

The Green racket’s buying area has a gift-note field and quantity choice. The Vintage Green cap is in stock at £25, with no option to add it beside the racket’s buying button.

One changeTest an optional £25 Vintage Green cap choice beside the Green racket’s buying button. Keep the racket available on its own and compare revenue per visitor against the current page.
