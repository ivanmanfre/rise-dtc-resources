02 / Paid ads

Match the landing headline to the sensitive-skin ad

Your ad states "Formulato per la pelle sensibile, con aloe e burro di karité". In the recorded landing page text we read, razor burn appears only in a customer note, and the ad's sensitive-skin message is not repeated.

One change

Keep the ad unchanged and send its clicks to two page versions: the current one, and one with only a new headline, such as "Il rasoio pensato per la pelle sensibile." Use only skin claims your store already states, such as "Dermatologist approved for sensitive skin" on your starter kit page.

03 / Your store

Test a travel case option on the blades page

Your blades page says "Add a razor case for your razor to keep your blades safe and sharp". The Travel Case is a separate product at 6,95 EUR.

One change

Compare the current blades page with a version that adds a Travel Case option next to the frequency choice, with copy such as "Keep your razor dry and safe in your bag". Check what the current page already offers first, and only change what differs.
