01 / Proposed paid test

Test a morning moisturizer opening line in new ads against an ingredients line

Your homepage opens on "For the people who check every ingredient" and already pairs moisturizer with sun protection. Your FAQ says some of your most enthusiastic customers are women in their fifties and sixties using the balm as a morning moisturizer, a different group new ads could lead with.

One change

Make two new ads with the same visuals, audience and product page, changing only the opening line. If you target women 50 and over, run both ads to that same group. Draft A: "A rich morning moisturizer with mineral sun protection built in." Draft B: "Mineral sun protection in six ingredients you can pronounce." Both lines are untested proposals.

Your homepage opens on "For the people who check every ingredient" and already pairs moisturizer with sun protection. Your FAQ says some of your most enthusiastic customers are women in their fifties and sixties using the balm as a morning moisturizer, a different group new ads could lead with.

What to measure
Winner: first-order revenue per ad dollar, same audience, budget and window, split so each person sees one ad. Also watch: purchases, click-through rate. Safety: refund rate after 30 days.

02 / Your store

State exactly what the 3-or-more pack buys

A shopper choosing the 3-or-more tile can't tell what that price buys. The tile reads SAVE 40% with cancel anytime, but $75.60 against $108.00 is 30% off, and the tile does not say whether it is a one-off order or a repeating delivery.

One change

Rewrite the tile so it states the saving its own prices show, whether it is a one-time order or a subscription and, if it repeats, how the delivery schedule is set. Keep the prices and layout unchanged.

03 / Your store

Split paid clicks between homepage and product page

The homepage buy box we saw offers a quantity box, while the balm product page offers 1, 2 and 3-or-more pack tiles. If you run ads, which page earns more first-order revenue per click is an open decision.

One change

Once the 3-or-more tile is fixed, send one audience's ad clicks through a split-URL tool, or run two identical ads in a platform split test, so each visitor is randomly sent to the homepage or the balm product page. Keep the ad, offer and audience the same so only the landing page changes.

04 / Your store

Show free standard shipping in the homepage buy box

The homepage gives shoppers two shipping messages. The buy box says shipping is calculated at checkout, while the same page promises free U.S. shipping on every order, and your shipping policy says standard shipping is free.

One change

Replace the checkout line in the homepage buy box with "Free standard U.S. shipping". The word standard keeps room for the paid founder delivery option.
