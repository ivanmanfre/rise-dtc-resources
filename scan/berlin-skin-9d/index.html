01 / Proposed paid test

Test a sample-first ad message for Carrot Seed Serum

First-time buyers cannot return a full-size product, because your policy says all sales are final. Your FAQ offers 2 deluxe samples with each order, and one reviewer says a sample won them over, so trying before committing may be worth testing as the opening message.

One change

Proposed new ad test, same Carrot Seed Serum page for both: Ad A reads 'Unsure about a serum? Every order includes 2 deluxe samples,' Ad B reads 'Carrot Seed Serum: carrot seed vitamin A for a more even look.' First confirm whether samples are added automatically or on request.

03 / Your store

Test a repeat-delivery option on Blue Tansy Cream

The sampled Blue Tansy Cream page sells single jars by quantity, with no repeat-delivery choice shown. One reviewer says they keep an extra jar on hand, so an easy way to stay stocked may be worth testing.

One change

For half of visitors, add a 'deliver again' choice beside Add to cart with a modest repeat discount, adapting Naturopathica's autoship layout as an example, not a benchmark. Set the interval from your actual reorder gap for this jar.

Full test plan & sources +

What we saw
The sampled Blue Tansy Cream page sells single jars by quantity, with no repeat-delivery choice shown. One reviewer says they keep an extra jar on hand, so an easy way to stay stocked may be worth testing.

What to measure
Winner: total revenue per page visitor, including repeat orders, both groups, same window, run for at least the longest delivery interval. Also check margin after the discount.

Test setup
For half of visitors, add a 'deliver again' choice beside Add to cart with a modest repeat discount, adapting Naturopathica's autoship layout as an example, not a benchmark. Set the interval from your actual reorder gap for this jar.
