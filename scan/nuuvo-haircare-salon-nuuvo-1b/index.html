Offer the shampoo and conditioner set beside shampoo sizes

Your Nourishing Shampoo page says to "Follow with healing conditioner", and the shampoo and conditioner rank #2 and #3 in your best-selling sort. Yet the set ($60, versus $64 for the 8 oz shampoo and conditioner bought separately) appears only under "You may also like" further down the page, away from the size picker.

One change

Add a choice under the shampoo size picker: shampoo alone, or the shampoo and conditioner set (the $60 16 oz set for 8 oz, the $19 2 oz set for 2 oz). Show the line "Shampoo and conditioner, made to be used together." and link to the matching set.

What we saw
Your Nourishing Shampoo page says to "Follow with healing conditioner", and the shampoo and conditioner rank #2 and #3 in your best-selling sort. Yet the set ($60, versus $64 for the 8 oz shampoo and conditioner bought separately) appears only under "You may also like" further down the page, away from the size picker.

What to measure
Randomly split Nourishing Shampoo page visitors between current page and new choice. Over the same period compare revenue per visitor; also watch set share, order margin and refunds.
