01 / Paid ads

Test the page's 17.5% saving in Lion's Mane ads

Your Lion's Mane ad ends with 'save up to 15%', but the page it lands on offers up to 17.5% off plus free gifts from the second order. The ad may be underselling the offer buyers actually get.

One change

Run two versions of the ad with the same image and page: the current closing line, and one ending with: 'Subscribe and save up to 17.5%, plus free gifts from your 2nd order.'

What we saw
Your Lion's Mane ad ends with 'save up to 15%', but the page it lands on offers up to 17.5% off plus free gifts from the second order. The ad may be underselling the offer buyers actually get.

What to measure
Purchases and first-order revenue per pound of ad spend for both versions over the same window and budget split, plus subscription share and margin. Judge second orders after 10 weeks.

Test setup
Run two versions of the ad with the same image and page: the current closing line, and one ending with: 'Subscribe and save up to 17.5%, plus free gifts from your 2nd order.'

02 / Paid ads

Make the Cordyceps strength ratio match ad and page

The Cordyceps ad states a 1:5 ratio, while its product page lists 4:1 near the top and 1:4 further down, beside a 'Grown In The UK' strip on a China-sourced product. Your FAQ explains that the ratio's second number changes the strength, so these need one agreed figure.

One change

Agree one ratio from your lab results and use it in the ad and both places on the Cordyceps page. Swap the shared badge strip there for one that matches this product's sourcing and alcohol-free recipe.

03 / Paid ads

Test the felled-tree rescue story on Lion's Mane ads

Your Lion's Mane ad opens with a UK-native sourcing claim, but not the rescue story your homepage tells about a felled tree. The homepage names a beech while the ad's landing page names an oak, so that needs agreeing first.

One change

First make the homepage and the Lion's Mane page name the same tree. Then keep the image and destination and swap the opener for: 'Our Lion's Mane was a wild find near our farm. When its tree was felled, we rescued a piece from the ground and grew our clone from it.'

04 / Your store

Add a dose-to-delivery guide beside subscription frequency

The Lion's Mane subscription menu lists 'Deliver every 4 weeks' first, while the page states up to a 50 day supply per bottle. Your FAQ says 25 to 50 days depending on dose, but the frequency menu itself gives no pointer to match the two.

One change

Show half of Lion's Mane visitors one line under the frequency menu: 'At 1ml a day a bottle lasts about 50 days, so choose every 6 or 8 weeks. At 2ml it lasts about 25 days, so set quantity to 2 bottles every 6 or 8 weeks. Both leave room for the short breaks our FAQ suggests.' The other half see the page as now.
