01 / Paid ads

Test sending the preschool ad straight to Ages 3–5

The preschool ad names ages 3–5 but sends shoppers to the homepage, which already links to the Ages 3–5 collection. A direct link would save shoppers a click to browse the collection, which lists the Beginners Pack and the Paddington alphabet bundle.

One change

After the Black Friday offer ends, use one test version of the preschool video ad without Black Friday messaging, keeping the Christmas gift line only if it is still timely. Randomly send people who click that same ad to either the homepage or the Ages 3–5 collection.

02 / Paid ads

Test writing stage against screen-free gifting

The choice is whether to lead with a screen-free gift or help choosing the next writing stage. The recorded Best Sellers ad includes both ideas after its Black Friday opening.

One change

On the same image and Best Sellers destination, test “A screen-free gift for little writers” against “First marks, alphabet practice or joined-up letters? Pick their next step.” Remove the Black Friday lines from both versions and keep all other copy the same.

Collapse extras on the Beginners Pack page
