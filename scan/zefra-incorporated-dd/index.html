01 / Paid ads

Send golf-cart ads to your golf-cart page, not the product page

All six ads we read, including your golf-cart ad ("Clip it to a compatible golf cart rail with the included C-Clamp"), link to the MistOne product page. Your golf-cart page answers "Will it fit my golf cart?" with rail and cup-holder guidance, photo help and its own buy button.

One change

Untested proposal: duplicate one golf-cart ad and change only where its BLACKMIST20 discount link sends people, from the MistOne product page to your golf-cart page. Keep the video, text, audience and budget the same, split viewers evenly at random and run both until the sale ends November 29.

02 / Your store

Add a fit check and photo help to the Cup Holder page

Cup Holder fit is a buying decision: your golf-cart page asks buyers to check depth and fit and offers photo help, while the Cup Holder product page describes a universal fit for standard cup holders. Repeating that fit guidance where the mount is bought may help hesitant buyers order with confidence.

One change

Untested proposal: randomly show half of Cup Holder page visitors a short note near Add to cart; the other half see the current page. Draft: "Check your cup holder's depth and fit before ordering. Not sure? Send us a photo of your setup and we'll help you choose."

What we saw
Cup Holder fit is a buying decision: your golf-cart page asks buyers to check depth and fit and offers photo help, while the Cup Holder product page describes a universal fit for standard cup holders. Repeating that fit guidance where the mount is bought may help hesitant buyers order with confidence.

What to measure
Cup Holder orders not returned within 30 days, per Cup Holder page visitor, counting orders placed anywhere on the site, same window. Also check: fit support contacts and gross margin.

Test setup
Untested proposal: randomly show half of Cup Holder page visitors a short note near Add to cart; the other half see the current page. Draft: "Check your cup holder's depth and fit before ordering. Not sure? Send us a photo of your setup and we'll help you choose."
