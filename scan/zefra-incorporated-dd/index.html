01 / Paid ads

Test the golf-gift ad on the MistOne product page

Your golf-gift video ad is the only one of the eight we read without the Black Friday discount link, and its text mentions no sale and says "Shop now at Zefra.com or find us on Amazon." It opens the shop page, which adds a 25% email offer, so these clicks see a different offer from the other ads.

One change

Untested proposal: duplicate the golf-gift ad as a Meta split test with equal budgets and dates, one copy linking to the shop page and one to the MistOne product page. Keep text and video the same; both pages show the automatic 20% offer, and only the shop page adds a 25% email welcome offer.

02 / Paid ads

Test a price-first golf-cart ad against Save 20%

The golf-cart Black Friday ad text says to save 20% but gives no price, though one other ad we read states $119.20. The MistOne product page shows $119.20 against $149.00 with the C-Clamp included, so this ad leaves out a price the store already prints.

One change

Change only the ad text and keep the video, page and offer the same. Variant B text: "Black Friday: MistOne with C-Clamp included, $119.20, down from $149.00, through November 29. Clip it to a compatible cart rail and aim the breeze."

03 / Proposed paid test

Test a Tripod ad for past MistOne buyers

MistOne comes with one C-Clamp, and your home page suggests adding an optional Tripod for camp tables and parked RVs. The Tripod is a separate, lower-priced item with its own page, which makes it a possible second purchase after the fan.

One change

If you hold a consented list of past MistOne buyers, run a new Meta ad to that list with a random no-ad holdout, linking to the Tripod page. Draft: "Bought a MistOne? Add a Tripod for the camp table. $23.20 after 20% off."

What we saw
MistOne comes with one C-Clamp, and your home page suggests adding an optional Tripod for camp tables and parked RVs. The Tripod is a separate, lower-priced item with its own page, which makes it a possible second purchase after the fan.
