01 / Your store

Fix the homepage NMN price, then test showing the subscribe price

Your homepage card lists the NMN complex at £24.99, while the collection page and the product page show £27.99. Shoppers who start on the homepage therefore see a lower price than the one at the buying button.

One change

Correct the homepage NMN card to £27.99 to match the collection and product pages, then randomise homepage visitors between that plain card and one reading '£27.99, or £25.19 when you subscribe'. Confirm on the call that £27.99 is the intended price, and whether the homepage '£24.99 per bottle' block needs the same fix.

02 / Proposed paid test

Test focus message against long-term ageing message

Brain Longevity Complex is your only product in stock, so it is what paid traffic can sell now. We found no active Google ads linked to your domain, so we cannot see which of its four page themes you lead with: afternoon focus, clear thinking, energy or long-term ageing.

One change

Proposed new ad test, not an observed live ad. Run two Meta image ads to the same Brain Longevity Complex page, changing only the headline, with the same audience. A: "Focus that holds past 3pm? Six actives, SGS tested." B: "Sharp at 40 should still mean sharp at 60. NMN plus five supporting actives." Check both headlines against the authorised health claims you rely on before running.

03 / Your store

Test a simpler NMN subscribe box layout

The NMN subscribe box lists a 10% discount, free shipping and two cashback figures, £2.75 and £3.24. The box text does not say how they relate, so a clearer layout is worth testing.

One change

Split NMN page visitors. One half sees the current box. The other sees 'Subscribe: 10% off, free shipping, pause or cancel anytime', with cashback on one separate line using the amount you confirm.

04 / Your store

Send sold out Magnesium and Omega visitors to the NMN complex

Your best-selling page marks two of your three products 'Sold Out': Magnesium and Vegan Omega. Their product pages still print Add to Cart, so shoppers may reach a button they cannot use.

One change

Randomise visitors to the sold out Magnesium and Omega pages between the current page and one that replaces Add to Cart with a clear sold out note and a link to Brain Longevity Complex, your one product in stock. Confirm restock dates on the call, and keep paid traffic off these pages until restocked.
