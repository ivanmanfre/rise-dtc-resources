Finding 1 · Shopper paths

No express checkout button on Save The Trees Hat

A shopper on Save The Trees Hat has to add to cart, open the cart and start checkout before they can pay. There's no one-tap button for shoppers with a saved Shop Pay or wallet account.

Recommended next step

Turn on dynamic checkout buttons in your theme's product page settings.

Finding 2 · Repeat purchase

We didn't find a rewards program on your store

A first-time buyer earns no points or credit toward a next order, so nothing on the store itself gives them a reason to come back.

Recommended next step

Add a simple points or next-order credit program and mention it on the order confirmation page.
