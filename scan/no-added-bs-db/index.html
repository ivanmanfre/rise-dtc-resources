01 / Proposed paid test

Test ingredient-trust versus scent-and-feel ad messages for the shampoo

Your shampoo page leads on ingredient trust, stating it is "rated a 100/100 on Yuka". Several reviewers also single out the scent, one writing "Left my hair smelling amazing", so both are real angles for a new ad.

One change

Run two new ad messages to the same single-shampoo page. Draft A: "Shampoo with no added BS, rated 100/100 on Yuka, with every ingredient explained." Draft B: "The natural shampoo reviewers say smells amazing and leaves hair feeling clean."

What we saw
Your shampoo page leads on ingredient trust, stating it is "rated a 100/100 on Yuka". Several reviewers also single out the scent, one writing "Left my hair smelling amazing", so both are real angles for a new ad.

What to measure
Winner: first-order purchases per ad dollar, one randomly split audience, equal budgets, same full weeks, preset minimum purchases. Also check revenue and margin after costs. Clicks are a side signal.

02 / Your store

Test a 1 or 2 bottle choice on the shampoo page

Your store sells one bottle at $21.99 and a 2 pack at $38.99 on separate pages. The single bottle page carries the reviews, while the 2 pack page still says "Be the first to review!", so the larger option sits on a page with less proof.

One change

On the single bottle page, add a bottle-count choice above Add to cart: 1 bottle for $21.99 or the 2 pack for $38.99, so both options sit beside the existing reviews. Draft label: "Stock up: 2 bottles for $38.99, save $4.99."

03 / Your store

Test a review-led benefit summary near the shampoo buy button

Your shampoo page says its L-Arginine is known to "encourage faster, stronger hair growth", but what reviewers describe, such as "My hair felt very clean", sits far below the buy button. Shoppers see the promise before the proof.

One change

Add a short summary near the buy button of what reviewers mention, linked to the reviews already lower on the page, and keep the existing growth and ingredient copy unchanged. Draft: "What customers say: clean, soft, great-smelling."
