01 / Proposed paid test

Test Yuka-trust versus scent ad messages

Your shampoo page leads on ingredient trust and states it is "rated a 100/100 on Yuka". Reviewers also praise scent, such as "Smells amazing and works even better.", so there are two distinct angles for a new ad.

One change

Run two new ad messages to the same single-shampoo page, changing only the message. Draft A: "Shampoo with no added BS, rated 100/100 on the Yuka app, with every ingredient explained." Draft B: "The natural shampoo reviewers say smells amazing and leaves hair feeling clean."

What we saw
Your shampoo page leads on ingredient trust and states it is "rated a 100/100 on Yuka". Reviewers also praise scent, such as "Smells amazing and works even better.", so there are two distinct angles for a new ad.

What to measure
Winner: first-order purchases per ad dollar, equal budgets, random audience split, same full weeks, preset minimum purchases. Check revenue and margin after ad cost. Clicks are a side signal.

02 / Your store

Offer a 2 pack choice on bottle page

A single bottle is $21.99 and the 2 pack is $38.99, each on its own page. The single bottle page shows five reviews, while the 2 pack page says "Be the first to review!", so the larger option has less visible proof.

One change

On the single bottle page, add a choice above Add to cart: 1 bottle at $21.99 or the existing 2 pack at $38.99. Draft label: "Stock up: 2 bottles for $38.99." Compare against today's page.

What we saw
A single bottle is $21.99 and the 2 pack is $38.99, each on its own page. The single bottle page shows five reviews, while the 2 pack page says "Be the first to review!", so the larger option has less visible proof.

What to measure

03 / Your store

Add review summary beside shampoo buy button

On the sampled shampoo page, the store's growth claim, "encourage faster, stronger hair growth", and a long ingredient list come before the reviews. Reviews such as "My hair felt very clean" sit much lower, so shoppers see the promise before the proof.

One change

Add a one-line summary beside Add to cart, linked to the reviews already on the page, and leave all other copy unchanged. Draft: "Reviewers mention a clean feel, soft hair and a good scent."
