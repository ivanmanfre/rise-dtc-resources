Finding 1 · Shopper paths

12.1% of your products are sold out while your 56 Meta ads run

You have 56 active ads on Meta, and 12.1% of your products read out of stock right now. A click that lands on a sold-out option can't turn into an order.

Recommended next step

Pull sold-out products out of your ads and product feed until they're back in stock.

Finding 2 · Shopper paths

No pay-in-installments option on your product pages

Your typical product costs ₹1,200, and the product page offers no way to split that into smaller payments, so the full price is the only number a shopper sees.

Recommended next step

Turn on a pay-in-installments option at checkout and show it under the price.
