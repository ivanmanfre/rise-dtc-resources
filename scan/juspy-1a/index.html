01 / Paid ads

Fix the blend ad's ingredient line, then test its landing page

Your blend ad names "12 nutrient-rich ingredients including cacao, collagen, reishi and turmeric". The blend page we read lists its ingredients without reishi or turmeric and says "That’s it. Nothing else." A visitor drawn by those ingredients could find a different list once they open the blend page.

One change

Confirm the current formula, then fix the ad's ingredient line in both versions. Draft: "Marine collagen, organic cacao, pea and rice protein and ashwagandha in one chocolate blend." Then, as an untested proposal, split the ad's audience evenly: half land on the current eight-item shop page, half on the blend page, which shows the full ingredient list.

02 / Paid ads

Test a how-to-take-it creatine ad against the proof-claim ad

Your creatine ad states "Clinically proven. Zero filler." and mentions a daily sachet. Your landing page explains the routine: stir into water or juice, 5g once daily. A variant that leads with that routine may fit the click better.

One change

Split the same audience between the current creatine ad and a variant that leads with the routine, both sending to the same creatine page. Draft: "One sachet a day. Stir 5g of creatine into water or juice. Juspy Creatine, 30 sachets."

03 / Your store

Add a pack-size guide based on daily use

Size choice is unclear on your blend page. It says the 400g pack "offers up to 20 servings", but also says to "Use 40g per shake or 20g in coffee". At 40g a day, 400g lasts 10 days, and the picker lists "200g (Travel Pack) 600g 800g" plus a monthly plan.

One change

Add one line under the pack size choices: "Daily shake (40g): 400g lasts 10 days, 800g lasts 20. Daily coffee scoop (20g): 400g lasts 20 days." Half of visitors see the current page and half see the page with the new line.
