01 / Paid ads

Make your clearance ads' money-back promise match your Terms

Your Soul Discovery, Duo and four-box ads we read promise a "30-day money-back guarantee", yet your Terms say the Soul Promise "excludes clearance products" and bulk or multiple Soul System orders, and your sale page says "All sales are final." Buyers at $35, $60 or $99.95 are promised a refund your own terms exclude, which invites disputes.

One change

Before more spend, choose one rule and make your ads, product pages and Terms agree. Either honour the guarantee on clearance Soul Discovery, Duo and Bundle orders and update the Terms, or remove the money-back promise from clearance ads and pages and say "All sales are final."

02 / Paid ads

Send clearance ad clicks to the Soul Discovery page

This clearance ad offers Soul Discovery at $35 or two for $60, yet sends people to the sale collection, where seven of the 13 listings show Sold Out. The collection does list the Bundle, Duo and Soul Discovery first, so the open question is whether a single product page turns these clicks into more revenue.

One change

This is a route test: keep the ad's offer the same and send half its clicks to the Soul Discovery product page, with one line beside the price linking to the Duo: "Or get two systems for $60." Start once its "30-Day Guarantee" wording matches your Terms, as in the first finding, and before the sale ends 30 November.

03 / Paid ads

Show Soul Discovery reviews on the four-box Bundle page

Your four-box ads send people to the Bundle page, which shows 0 Reviews, while the single Soul Discovery and Duo pages both show 156 Reviews for the same box. Visitors are asked to buy four boxes with no buyer feedback visible on that page.

One change

On the Bundle page, show the Soul Discovery review count and a few of its reviews, labelled clearly as reviews of the single Soul Discovery box the bundle contains. Start once the guarantee wording in the first finding is fixed.

04 / Proposed paid test

Film a customer's pre-video-call mask routine for Soul Discovery

A customer review on the Reset & Revitalise mask page describes a specific scene to film. Sallyanne masks before a Teams call when working from home, then uses the leftover serum on their neck, chest, arms and hands. Soul Discovery includes four hydrogel masks.

One change

Film a 15-second video of that scene: mask on, laptop open, leftover serum pressed onto neck and hands, ending on the Soul Discovery box. Draft line: "Mask on before the 9am call. The leftover serum goes on my neck and hands." Save it for your next Soul Discovery stock, not this clearance, whose Reset & Revitalise masks are dated 11 October and whose sale ends 30 November.
