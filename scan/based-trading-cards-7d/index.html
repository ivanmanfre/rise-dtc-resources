01 / Proposed paid test

Test an unboxing video ad for the Experience Box

Your Experience Box tops your best-selling list at $1,799, but its car and Bitcoin prize ended November 1, 2026, so ads must sell the contents, handcrafted and limited to 420. An unboxing video may show them, like the black and gold scissors your Shark Bundle page describes, better than a photo.

One change

Once the CAD installment fix is live, build two new test ads to the Experience Box page, since no live Google ads were found: a 20 to 30 second unboxing video where hands cut the seal with the black and gold scissors and lift out each item, and a box photo. Both use 'Limited to 420. Every piece handcrafted.' and never present the Golden Key as a prize chance.

02 / Your store

Fix the CAD installment amount on the Experience Box

Your Experience Box lists at $1,799.00 USD, but the Payment options block and the Q&A both quote Shop Pay as 4 payments of $845.15 CAD. Four of those total $3,380.60 CAD, while the price is $1,799 USD, so a buyer cannot tell what each payment will be.

One change

Replace both hard-coded CAD lines, in the Payment options block and the Q&A answer, with Shop Pay's live installment message, or a checked US dollar figure, so the page and checkout show the same amount.

04 / Your store

Say which Family Bundle packs hold the chase hits

Your Family Bundle page says everyone at the table gets in on the chase, and the rare chase hits it lists come with the 2 OPP Series 3 packs. Your Genesis page says Genesis packs carry no holo hits. With all sales final, naming which packs hold the hits sets expectations before purchase.

One change
