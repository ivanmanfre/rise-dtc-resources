01 / Paid ads

Test the Swedish page for your award and testimonial ads

Nine of your 12 active ads offer free first-order shipping with code AWARD but link to a VITALIZE page that, as read, is in English and says shipping costs are shown at checkout. The two blood-measurement ads already use the Swedish page, which repeats the code, and other store pages say free EU shipping, so the code's value is unclear.

One change

Your RENEW and collection pages say free EU shipping, so first confirm whether a Swedish order without the code already ships free. Then run a Meta A/B test duplicating these nine ads with the same copy in both, one version linking to the current page and one to the Swedish page, finishing before the AWARD offer ends on 30 November.

02 / Paid ads

Test the measurement claim against a testimonial as the ad lead

The 11 store-linked ads we read lead with three messages: the award, energy testimonials, and a claim that every customer measured saw blood NAD⁺ rise at least 50%. Both measurement ads say this is not a clinical study, but lack the AWARD offer and use another page, so their results are not yet comparable with the testimonial ads.

One change

After the shipping check, run a Meta A/B test: a copy of one video Theo L. testimonial ad against a copy of the measurement ad with this line added: "Fri frakt på första beställningen med koden AWARD. Gäller t.o.m. 30 november." Keep the "Inte en klinisk studie" wording, check the claim against Meta's health-claim rules and point both to the Swedish page, so mainly the lead message differs.

03 / Your store

Offer the VITALIZE + RENEW bundle on the VITALIZE page

Your VITALIZE page says "Pair with RENEW (PM) for 24-hour cellular support" in its how-to-take guidance, and your FAQ advises starting with VITALIZE and adding RENEW later. The VITALIZE purchase area, as read, does not offer the FAQ's Longevity Essentials bundle, so the test checks whether offering it upfront helps or hurts first orders.

One change

Randomly show half of VITALIZE page visitors an optional line under the monthly routine button, "Add RENEW for evening repair", linking to your existing Longevity Essentials bundle at its listed price. The other half see the page unchanged.

04 / Your store

Link the NAD+ Tracking Pack from the energy starter card

Your collection page's "Start with energy" card says VITALIZE is "measurable within the first month" but links only to VITALIZE. The NAD+ Tracking Pack, offering "a baseline, a supported interval with VITALIZE, and a retest", sits lower on the page, apart from the three start options where shoppers choose.

One change

Randomly show half of collection page visitors the "Start with energy" card with one added line under its VITALIZE link: "Optional: track your NAD+ before and after", linking to the NAD+ Tracking Pack, while the rest see the current card. Check the pack's price and options first, as we did not read its page.
