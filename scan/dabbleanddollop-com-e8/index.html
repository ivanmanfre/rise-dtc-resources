01 / Paid ads

Test a routine-led version of your baby ad

Your baby ad leads with ingredient count, but its collection promises "your complete baby routine, from bubbles to bedtime", with gift sets at $39.99 and $74.99. Other baby items cost $13.99 to $15.99, so a routine message may lead shoppers to add items toward your $49.95 free-shipping line.

From your public ad

“10 ingredients or fewer, fragrance-free formulas”
View the actual ad ↗
One changeKeep the baby collection as the destination. Run the current ingredient-led ad against a routine-led version in a Meta A/B test, changing only the primary text. Draft: "Bubbles to bedtime, all in one. One fragrance-free baby routine, from bath to lotion."
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
Sales per dollar of ad spend, compared between the two ads. Also watch items per order and the share of orders reaching $49.95, from orders tagged by each ad.

02 / Paid ads

Send your mix-and-match ad straight to the Custom Bath Gift Set

Your ad promises seven scents to "make their own" and lands on the gift sets collection, which offers "mixable fun with curated sets" across eleven listed products. Your Custom Bath Gift Set page, listed in that collection, uses the ad's own words: "mix, match, and make bath time their own".

From your public ad

“Seven scents to mix, match and make their own”
View the actual ad ↗
One changeThis is a route test, not a copy test. Run the ad with unchanged copy as a Meta A/B test, splitting traffic between the current gift sets collection and the Custom Bath Gift Set page. If this ad targets families buying for themselves, run the same test with the Custom Bath Bundle page instead.

Purchases per 1,000 ad clicks and revenue per click, compared between the two routes. Also watch average order value and cost per purchase.

03 / Your store

Pre-fill a suggested trio in the custom gift set

Your Custom Bath Gift Set opens with "Choose Your 3 Bottles. This step is required" across twelve bottle options. Your store also sells pre-built sets ("Bath Gift Sets From $29.97"), and your homepage suggests a three-product "Get This Bath Time Blend".

One changeInside the build-your-own flow, show half of visitors three suggested bottles already selected, labelled "Our starter trio, swap any bottle". The other half see the current empty picker. Choose the suggested scents from your own sales data.

04 / Your store

Test a Detangling Duo card on the conditioner page

Your conditioner page's pairing block shows only the Leave-In Spray Detangler, while your Cherry on Top® Detangling Duo Bundle ($22.99) sits elsewhere in the store.

One changeIf the Detangling Duo is the conditioner plus the spray (its $22.99 price sits just under the $23.98 the two cost separately), show half of visitors a bundle card in place of the spray card, worded "Get both together for $22.99". The other half see the current spray card.
