01 / Proposed paid test

Test a clothing-versus-tester-paper video ad for #9

Online shoppers have to choose your new #9 Vanilla & Oudh without smelling it. Its one product review, from verified reviewer maya, says it smells better on clothes than on tester paper, which is a specific scene you can film.

One change

Proposed, untested: run a new Meta video ad showing #9 on a fabric sleeve beside a tester strip, with the text "A verified reviewer says it smells better on clothes than on tester paper." Compare it with a plain product-shot ad that uses the same audience, budget and #9 destination.

02 / Your store

Fix returns page, then test a returns note

Shoppers checking your refund page see the placeholder [INSERT RETURN ADDRESS] where the address should be. The page offers 30-day returns but excludes personal care goods such as beauty products, without naming fragrances, so shoppers must guess whether an unopened perfume can come back.

One change

Replace the placeholder and add one sentence stating whether unopened fragrances can be returned. Then show half of #5 page visitors a line under Add to cart, such as "Returns: [your rule for unopened fragrances]. Questions? Message us." If fragrances cannot be returned, fix only the policy page.

03 / Your store

Show in-stock scents on the sold-out #7 page

Your #7 Floral, Citrus & Oudh page showed Sold out when we read it, yet it sits second in your best-selling sort. In the pages we read, #7, #5 and #9 showed no other scents to buy, while your Moonlight page shows a "You may also like" row.

One change

While #7 is out of stock, add a row of in-stock numbered Extrait de Parfums to the #7 page, using the same recommendation feature as Moonlight. Show it to half of #7 visitors and keep the current page for the rest.

Full test plan & sources +

What we saw
Your #7 Floral, Citrus & Oudh page showed Sold out when we read it, yet it sits second in your best-selling sort. In the pages we read, #7, #5 and #9 showed no other scents to buy, while your Moonlight page shows a "You may also like" row.

What to measure
Revenue per #7 page visitor for both halves over the same window. Clicks on the row are a diagnostic only. End the test if #7 returns to stock.

Test setup
While #7 is out of stock, add a row of in-stock numbered Extrait de Parfums to the #7 page, using the same recommendation feature as Moonlight. Show it to half of #7 visitors and keep the current page for the rest.
