Add a scent family chooser to the shop page

One change

Give each perfume a family, then add a row of scent buttons (Floral, Rose, Spicy, Amber, Leathery, Oudh, Fruity, Clean) above the product grid, each filtering the list, with the line "Not sure where to start? Pick a scent family." Compare this untested proposal with the current shop page.

What we saw
Extrait names list their notes, such as "Mild Spicy, Nutty, Woody", but the best seller Moonlight, plus Jade and Snowflakes, have no scent words in their names. Moonlight's page shows no Fragrance Profile, unlike the two extrait pages we read, and the 36 products across three pages filter only by availability and price.

What to measure
Split shop-page visitors evenly between the two versions. Winner: purchases per visitor in each. Check order size, margin and ad visitors separately. Set the window on the call.

Test setup
Give each perfume a family, then add a row of scent buttons (Floral, Rose, Spicy, Amber, Leathery, Oudh, Fruity, Clean) above the product grid, each filtering the list, with the line "Not sure where to start? Pick a scent family." Compare this untested proposal with the current shop page.
