02 / Your store

Put the one-bottle offer on the bundle button

The 9oz bottle page shows 10% off one bottle in the product image, while its button says “Bundle & Save 40%”. Selecting one 9oz bottle in the builder showed £31.49 down from £34.99, a £3.50 saving.

One change

First confirm that one 9oz bottle from the builder costs £31.49 in the cart. Then test “Save 10% on one bottle or up to 40% on a bundle” against the current button, keeping its placement and link.
