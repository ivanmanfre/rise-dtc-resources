01 / Paid ads

Open the hot-sleeper video with a window scene

The hot-sleeper ad's text opens on waking up hot and sweaty, a feeling that is hard to picture on screen. A homepage review from Dani T, shown as via WhatsApp, gives a concrete scene to film: years of sleeping with the window open, even in winter.

One change

With Dani T's permission, make a version of the hot-sleeper video whose first five seconds show someone shutting a bedroom window on a winter night, with the review's words on screen: "For years I've been sleeping with my window open, even in winter". Run it as a Meta split test against the current video, with the same audience, budget, copy and homepage destination.

02 / Paid ads

Add the 30-night trial line to the price ad

The ad that admits the sleepwear is expensive explains the price, but its text has no trial or refund line. Your Maureen review ad already pairs price doubt with the 30-night trial, so this tests the same pairing in your own voice.

One change

Run the current price ad against a copy with one closing line added: "Try it for 30 nights. Love it or your money back." Keep the images, audience and homepage destination identical, so only the message changes.

03 / Paid ads

Add free UK delivery to the founder ad

The founder-story video's offer list has only free sleep gifts and a no-risk 30-night trial, while other ads we read list free UK delivery on sets. The store states free UK delivery on sets, so this ad may leave the delivery question open for UK shoppers.

One change

Add one line to the founder ad's offer list: "Free UK delivery on every set." Keep the limited-batch line, video, audience and homepage destination the same, so delivery is the only change.
