Finding 1 · Shopper paths

6% of your variants are sold out while your 336 Meta ads run

You have 336 active ads on Meta, and 6% of your variants read out of stock right now. A click that lands on a sold-out option can't turn into an order.

Recommended next step

Pull sold-out products out of your ads and product feed until they're back in stock.

Out of stock right now: 6% of your variants. See this on your storefront

Your checkout and analytics aren't public, so we read the pages a shopper sees before checkout.

Finding 2 · Repeat purchase

We didn't find a rewards program on your store

A first-time buyer earns no points or credit toward a next order, so a reward is not one of the reasons they have to come back.

Recommended next step

Add a simple points or next-order credit program and mention it on the order confirmation page.

Rewards program: None found on your storefront. See this on your storefront

Your repeat-order rate isn't public, so we read what your store gives a first-time buyer to come back for.

Paid-media check

Testing sprawl with no consolidated winner

You have 336 active ads spread across 27 distinct angles. Testing that wide without a consolidated winner spreads budget thin and makes performance hard to read. Pruning to the proven angles and concentrating spend behind them is the lever.

Next account check

By day two we are cutting losers and scaling winners, managing to Total Profit rather than platform metrics.

Most of your creative is dynamic catalog

Of the 120 ads we sampled, 57.5% run in a dynamic catalog format. When most of the creative is feed-driven, product feed quality becomes the creative that decides performance. Tightening feed titles, imagery, and hero products is the fastest win here.
