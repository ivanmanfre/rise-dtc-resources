01 / Paid ads

Test a grow-with-me opening in the snow pants and bibs ad

Your snow pants and bibs ad lists eight features, and the grow-with-me system is seventh. A homepage review says an extended cuff let the bibs fit another year after 3+ inches of growth, so a growth-led opening may be worth comparing.

One change

Keep the image and ad link the same, and change only the opening copy. Control is the current feature list. Variant draft: "Snow bibs that grow with your kid. Mountain Town pants and bibs expand for next season." Keep the 15K waterproof line below it.

Your snow pants and bibs ad lists eight features, and the grow-with-me system is seventh. A homepage review says an extended cuff let the bibs fit another year after 3+ inches of growth, so a growth-led opening may be worth comparing.

02 / Paid ads

Test one hero jacket against the three-jacket ad

Your jackets ad calls its three kids' jackets "award-winning" but never names the award. The Down Town Puffy page cites Outside Magazine's Best Kids' Winter Gear of 2023, and it is unresolved whether one named, award-backed jacket earns more sales than a list of three.

One change

Keep the ad link the same, use an image showing the Down Town Puffy in both versions, and change only the copy. Variant draft: "Meet the Down Town Puffy, named Outside Magazine's Best Kids' Winter Gear of 2023. Recycled down warmth in 11 colors." Control stays the three-jacket list; first check Puffy stock by size and color, since its page shows a low-inventory notice.

03 / Your store

Test a matching bib add-on on the jacket page

The Mountain Town Winter Jacket page lists the bib at Mountain Town Winter Bib $180.00 only in its "You may also love" row, so the matching piece is a separate click. A homepage review on your site mentions the matching pants and jacket.

One change

On the jacket page, add an optional "Finish the snow set" choice under the size picker, offering the bib or pants in the matching size where the size charts align, and the same color only where one exists. Draft: "Finish the snow set: add the Mountain Town Winter Bib." Control keeps the current page.

04 / Your store

Offer a down care kit beside the puffy

The Down Town Puffy page gives wash steps, and the page text we read shows no care-kit choice by the size picker. Your home page lists Grangers Down Care Kit $26.25 as its own product, so pairing the two is worth comparing.

One change
