01 / Paid ads

Give one Little or Big rule for age five

Parents of a five-year-old get two different answers on which Eight Nights edition to buy. The story page sends a five-year-old still being read to toward Little, while the product page says to pick Big for any five-year-old, and your recorded ads send clicks to both pages.

One change

Put one rule on both pages. If 'still being read to' is your real guide, add that line to the product page; if Big is right for every five-year-old, update the story page to match.

Fix, not a test. Track Little and Big exchange requests and age questions to your inbox before and after, alongside pre-orders per product-page visitor, as background only.

02 / Paid ads

Test Eight Nights ads: story page versus product page

Which page turns an Eight Nights ad click into a pre-order is unresolved. Two recorded ads go to the story page and two to the product page, where shoppers choose box and age and can add a second seat, but the pairs also use different wording, so current results cannot isolate the page.

One change

Run one existing message, the 'You bring the blessings' ad, as a Meta split test with two otherwise identical ads using the same single creative. Apply the age-five rule fix first. Keep wording, image, audience and budget fixed; change only the link: story page versus product page.

03 / Paid ads

Test the 'comment candles' close against a direct pre-order close

Your two product-page ads end by asking people to comment for the link, though the ad already points to that page, so whether this extra step helps or costs pre-orders is unknown. Orders from links sent in replies can also slip past normal ad tracking.

One change

Duplicate the image ad that ends with the comment invitation. Keep the image, text, audience and product-page link fixed; change only the last line. Control keeps the comment invitation; variant reads 'Tap Pre-order to reserve yours before the first candle.'

04 / Proposed paid test

Test a Round Trip flight-home ad against one-box ad

Whether paid ads should sell one box or the two-box flight-home pair is an open offer decision. The Little Passenger, Young Explorer and Ready to Fly pages already offer the Round Trip, a second box with different contents, and in the US two boxes clear the free-shipping line.

One change

Run a Meta split test: two ads to the Little Passenger page, your best seller, changing only the message. Control: 'Four wrapped parcels for their flight, opened one at a time after takeoff.' Variant: 'The flight home is the hard one. Add the Round Trip: a second box, different contents. Two boxes ship free in the US.'

Full test plan & sources +
