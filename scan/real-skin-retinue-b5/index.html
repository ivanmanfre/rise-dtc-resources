01 / Proposed paid test

Test Workday Versus Formula Ad Openings

The ad decision is whether to lead with what’s in the serum or where it fits in a routine. The product page explains the formula, while Mohammad’s homepage review gives the routine a concrete setting: outdoor work in the Texas heat.

One change

With permission, stage a work-bag packing scene inspired by Mohammad’s outdoor shifts, showing the serum and a separate sunscreen without suggesting he packed either. Run two new ads with identical footage and the same serum page, changing only the opening: “One daily serum for busy workdays. Not SPF.” versus “Antioxidants, ceramides and peptides in one serum. Not SPF.”

02 / Your store

Show Both Serum Result Stages Side by Side

The serum page says skin may feel softer after the first use, while the FAQ says skin tone may look more even over time and results vary. Would seeing both stages side by side help shoppers know what to expect before buying?

One change

Compare the current What to Expect block with the same copy displayed side by side in the same spot under its existing “From first application” and “With consistent use” labels. Keep the individual-results caveat and the note about self-reported feedback unchanged.

What we saw
The serum page says skin may feel softer after the first use, while the FAQ says skin tone may look more even over time and results vary. Would seeing both stages side by side help shoppers know what to expect before buying?

What to measure
Randomize eligible serum-page visitors within each traffic source. Compare completed serum purchases per assigned visitor over the same window; monitor refunds and ask both groups when they expect each change.

Test setup

03 / Your store

Pilot Scheduled Serum Delivery Without Extra Discounts

The repeat-order decision is whether scheduled delivery is useful without an extra discount. The serum page shows a quantity selector and cart button, while the FAQ describes subscriptions as a plan.

One change

Use order history to choose an interval, then pilot optional scheduled delivery beside the unchanged one-time default, with billing and cancellation terms upfront. Match per-bottle prices and shipping charges after promotions across both options on first orders and throughout the test, including renewals versus one-time reorders; if a promotion prevents a match, wait until it ends.

What we saw
The repeat-order decision is whether scheduled delivery is useful without an extra discount. The serum page shows a quantity selector and cart button, while the FAQ describes subscriptions as a plan.

What to measure
Randomize eligible visitors within traffic sources. Compare repeat serum revenue per assigned visitor over the same window spanning two scheduled renewals; check margin and first-order purchases separately.

Test setup
