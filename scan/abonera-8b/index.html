01 / Paid ads

Lead the personal-note starter ad with 10 pairs for 239 kr

The recorded starter-pack ad that opens with a personal note leads with the lowest price, a start pack from 99 kr, and on its landing page that price buys 5 pairs. The same page steers buyers toward 10 or 15 pairs, so a 10-pair lead could bring larger first orders.

One change

Keep the personal-note ad and its link as control. Add a variant that changes only the offer text: "Black Week: 10 par like bambussokker for 239 kr i første leveranse. Deretter 299 kr per påfyll. Ingen bindingstid, alltid fri frakt. Kun til 30. november."

02 / Paid ads

Show the gift ad's 1157 kr value on the gift page

The value a gift buyer is shown changes between ad and page. A recorded large gift card ad says 849 kr with a value of 1157 kr, while its destination shows 849 kr against 999 kr. The smaller gift card page already shows a value line beside its price.

One change

Under the price on the large gift card page, add one line matching the ad: "849 kr (verdi 1157 kr), 25 par i tre leveranser". Use it only if you can document the 1157 kr value, and leave the ad unchanged.

04 / Paid ads

Film the old-sock drawer clear-out as a video opening

The recorded text of the no-more-sock-chaos video ad promises matching socks in the mailbox but does not describe the moment of change. A home page review from Thomas M. describes happily throwing out all the old socks with the first delivery, a concrete scene worth filming.

One change

Keep the current video and its link as control. Add a video built on that review: old mismatched socks tipped from a drawer into a bag, then rows of matching pairs. Opening text: "Første levering kom. De gamle sokkene gikk rett ut." Offer text and link stay the same.

05 / Your store

Correct the 15-pair best-price label during Black Week

The start-pack picker labels 15 pairs as the best price per pair, but the Black Week cart prices in the banner above it say otherwise. At those prices 5 pairs for 99 kr is cheapest per pair, and 15 pairs for 359 kr costs slightly more per pair than 10 pairs for 239 kr.

One change

Until 30 November, replace the 15-pair label on both start-pack pages with one that stays true, such as "Flest par: bytt ut hele skuffen". Change nothing else on the pages.
