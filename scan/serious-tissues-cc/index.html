01 / Paid ads

Correct the tree-loss figure in the ad, then test it

One ad says 1.3 million trees a day are cut down, but your homepage says around a million a day, and 1.3M is your trees-planted figure. A mismatched number in an ad is easy for doubtful buyers to challenge.

One change

Change the line to your site's figure, for example: "Around a million trees a day are cut down for toilet roll. With 100% recycled, that number is zero." Then test it against a current carbon-comparison ad, same page and audience.

02 / Your store

Align the free trial's cancel day and delivery price

Your free trial page gives two cancel deadlines and two delivery prices. The timeline says cancel before day 15 while the FAQ says before day 14, and the order summary says £4.00 shipping while the FAQ says £3.95.

One change

Choose one cancel deadline, one first-box ship day and one delivery price, and use them in the timeline, order summary and FAQ. Fix this before sending any extra ad traffic to the trial.

03 / Your store

Show a labelled per-roll price beside each toilet roll price

A reviewer on your toilet roll page was still working out cost efficiency before subscribing. The default view we read shows one per-roll figure, £0.85, which matches the £38.25 subscription box, not the £42.50 one-time price.

One change

In the first view, before any option is picked, put a labelled per-roll price beside each price it shows: £42.50 for 45 rolls one-time (about 94p), £38.25 for 45 rolls on subscription (85p) and £105.00 for 135 rolls (about 78p). Keep everything else on the page the same.

04 / Your store

Offer laundry strips as a tick-box add-on to toilet roll

Some buyers already add other products to toilet roll: one reviewer tried laundry strips as an add-on, another added kitchen roll and tissues to a subscription. Kitchen roll deliveries are currently delayed, making it a weaker add-on to promote now.

One change

Add one optional tick-box beside Add to Cart on the toilet roll page: add a 50-wash pack of laundry strips to this order only, not to any subscription. Compare with the current page, which keeps its 'You may also like' row.

Full test plan & sources +
