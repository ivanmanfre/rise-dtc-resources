01 / Proposed paid test

Test FlipTwist versus engraving as the SWIGGER ad message

Among the SWIGGER page's selling points are the FlipTwist cap with two ways to drink, and engraving, which two reviews mention for birthday gifts. The pages we reviewed do not show which of these brings buyers, so the lead ad message is an open decision.

One change

Proposed new Meta ad test, not an observed ad. Run two ads to the same SWIGGER product page. Ad A: "Eén dop. Twee manieren. Rietje of schroefdop, jij kiest. Ontdek de SWIGGER." Ad B: "Jouw fles. Jouw naam erop. Laat 'm graveren." Only the message differs. We found no Meta pixel in the page source, so confirm or add one first or give both the same non-purchase goal.

02 / Your store

Offer The Shaker as the SWIGGER free-shipping add-on

On the SWIGGER page we reviewed, the bottle shows €29,90, below the €50 Dutch free-shipping line, and the "Je houdt misschien van" section suggests a €49,95 backpack, which clears €50 at €79,85. The shaker page already suggests the SWIGGER, and at €30,00 the pair clears €50 at €59,90, a smaller step.

One change

For Dutch visitors on the SWIGGER page, show the line 'Samen boven €50: gratis verzending in NL' under the suggestion in both versions, and swap the backpack for The Shaker™ in one. Keep price, options and personalisation unchanged, and check what your cart reward element already shows. Split visitors 50/50 between the backpack and shaker versions.

03 / Your store

Label each SWIGGER size by everyday use

The SWIGGER page shows its three sizes only as volumes and explains them in an FAQ answer about how much you carry and how often you refill. The homepage FAQ already gives each size a plainer role, which could help shoppers at the moment they pick a size.

One change

On the SWIGGER page, add a short use line for each size beside the size choice: '536 ml: compact, makkelijk mee', '710 ml: everyday-formaat', '946 ml: minder vaak bijvullen'. Leave price, colours and personalisation unchanged. Split visitors 50/50 between the current page and the labelled sizes.
