01 / Proposed paid test

Test variety versus low-price bag charm ad messages

Your bag charm page offers 16 designs and your homepage lists them from €9,00 EUR. We found no active Meta or Google ads, so nothing public shows yet whether variety or price wins a first purchase.

One change

Proposed new Meta ad test giving each version a random half of one audience, both linking to the Funky bag charms page. Version A: "Pick the charm that fits your mood. Cat, dog, elephant, heart." Version B: "Bag charms from €9. A small way to make any bag fun." Keep image style and budget identical. Use only in-stock designs and make sure a €9 option shows on the page.

What we saw

02 / Your store

Test a pick-any-three set on your €4 best sellers

Your top three best sellers, the Twisted candle, Mini flower hair clip and Bubble mini, are each €4,00 EUR. Shipping depends on the kind and weight bought, so one set of €4 items may make shipping feel better value than a single €4 item.

One change

Randomly split visitors to the Twisted candle, Mini flower hair clip and Bubble mini pages. The test version adds a card, "Pick any 3 of these €4 favourites for one set price", linking to a new set page for these three products, at a price you choose to protect margin after shipping. The control keeps the pages as they are.

03 / Your store

Show the charm combo beside single design choices

The bag charm page asks shoppers to pick one design from a long list. Your homepage already lists Funky bag charm combo's at €25,00 EUR, so the open question is whether showing it where the choice is made helps.

One change

Randomly split visitors to the Funky bag charms page. The test version adds a short card under the design picker, "See our Funky bag charm combo's", linking to the combo page, whose contents we did not check. The control keeps the page as it is.
