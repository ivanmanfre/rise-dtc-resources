01 / Paid ads

Test a holiday delivery line in the rpm video ad

Your rpm page says it is in stock in the US and Europe for the holidays, and your shipping policy says rpm orders ship within 2 business days and arrive in 1-5 days. None of the 12 live ad texts we read mentions either.

One change

Proposed, untested: in a Meta A/B test, run a copy of the "revelation for revolutions" video ad with the same video and landing page but new text. Draft: "In stock for the holidays. Ships within 2 business days and arrives in 1-5 days in the US and Europe." Run it against the current text before your holiday shipping cutoff, shown only to US and European audiences.

03 / Paid ads

Test wall versus bottom mount visuals in the one-motion ad

The one-motion ad copy does not say how the rpm is installed, and its dynamic format lets Meta choose which image to show. Your page sells wall-mounted and bottom-mounted versions, so public data cannot show which mount image drives purchases from this ad.

One change

Proposed, untested: in a Meta A/B test, run two single-image versions of the one-motion ad with identical copy and the same landing page, since the current dynamic ad lets Meta pick the image. One opens on a wall-mounted rpm, the other on a bottom-mounted one.

04 / Paid ads

Point the ad that talks about the rpm to the rpm page

Your live ad "Let the rpm change your perspective of what record storage can look like" links to the can page, which sells a made-to-order record crate. A shopper who clicks for the rpm lands on a different product.

One change

Check whether the can link is intended. If not, point this ad to the rpm page so the click lands on the product the copy names. This is a link fix, not an experiment.

05 / Paid ads

Make the can lead time match your shipping policy

Your can and eze pages say US orders currently ship in 4-7 weeks, but your shipping policy says 7-9 weeks. Several live can ads send buyers to the can page, so a buyer checking the policy before paying for a made-to-order crate sees two different lead times.

One change

Correct whichever lead time is out of date, so the can page, the eze page and the shipping policy state the same US and Europe delivery window next to Add to Cart. This is a consistency fix, not an experiment.

Full test plan & sources +

What we saw
Your can and eze pages say US orders currently ship in 4-7 weeks, but your shipping policy says 7-9 weeks. Several live can ads send buyers to the can page, so a buyer checking the policy before paying for a made-to-order crate sees two different lead times.

What to measure
A fix, not a test. Afterwards, watch the can page visit-to-order rate and lead-time questions before purchase as background only.

Test setup
Correct whichever lead time is out of date, so the can page, the eze page and the shipping policy state the same US and Europe delivery window next to Add to Cart. This is a consistency fix, not an experiment.
