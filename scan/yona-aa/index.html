01 / Paid ads

Test a solo setup video for the Cloud Bed

Two Bed 2.0 ads we read sell solo setup ('I even set it up without him'; 'without tools or a second pair of hands'). The two Australian Cloud Bed ad texts we read (not the video footage) lead with the Black Friday sale and skip setup, though the Cloud Bed page says 'One person can do it solo'.

One change

Untested idea: after Nov 27, test a new video against the current Cloud Bed video ad with its sale text removed, both to the Cloud Bed page. Film one person building the frame and fitting the cover alone, as a reviewer did in 20 minutes. Draft: 'Box to bed, one person, no tools. The Yona Cloud Bed clicks together, then the boucle cover pulls on.'

02 / Paid ads

Test a refund line in the reminder ad

The reminder ad pushes a November 27 deadline on a bed purchase, and its text says nothing about returns, though three Bed 2.0 ads we read mention the 30-night trial. The Cloud Bed page already offers a 30-night return. The refund policy adds that rural addresses charged shipping pay the same fee to return.

One change

Untested idea: run the current reminder ad against a copy that adds one refund line, both to the Cloud Bed page. Draft: 'Black Friday ends Nov 27. 20% off the Yona Cloud Bed. Try it for 30 nights, full refund if you don't love it.' Avoid free return shipping wording.

03 / Paid ads

Test a real-time first-build clip on the Bed 2.0 page

Your Bed 2.0 page promises '0 tools, 1 person, 15 minutes', but one reviewer on that page wrote that the build took longer than the ads suggested. Bed 2.0 ads send people to this page, so the setup expectation is set here before the first build.

One change

On the Bed 2.0 product page itself (not an ad), test a real-time clip of one first-time builder going from box to bed, placed near the 15-minute claim. Caption draft: 'First build? Here is the whole thing, start to finish.'

04 / Proposed paid test

Test a spare-room fold-away video for the Original bed

Your Original bed page says it unhooks and folds back into its box, and one reviewer packs it away when visitors leave. Another buyer described lifting the mattress onto the box first, then tipping it onto the bed, which is an easy scene to film.

One change

Film a new video ad: carry the Original in, build it, use the reviewer's box trick to get the mattress on, then fold the bed back into its box the next day. Draft: 'Guest bed tonight. Back in its box tomorrow. The Yona Bed Original folds away.'

05 / Your store

Fix which bed the $331 Bedroom Bundle includes

The bundle page does not make clear which bed the $331.00 price buys. Its model picker shows Yona Bed 2.0, but its includes list says Yona Bed (Original), so this needs settling before the 35% bundle is promoted harder.

One change

Make the includes list name the bed chosen in the model picker. State exactly which bed, bedhead and bedside table the $331.00 covers.
