Finding 1 · Repeat purchase

No bundle or multi-pack across your 10 products

A customer who likes one of your products has no bundle or multi-pack to step up to, so a second item has to be found and added by hand.

Recommended next step

Add one bundle built from your two best sellers, priced a little under buying them apart.

Finding 2 · Repeat purchase

We didn't find a rewards program on your store

A first-time buyer earns no points or credit toward a next order, so a reward is not one of the reasons they have to come back.

Recommended next step

Add a simple points or next-order credit program and mention it on the order confirmation page.
