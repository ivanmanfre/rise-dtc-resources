01 / Paid ads

Open your Vitamin D ad with the official autumn-and-winter guidance

The Vitamin D ad text we saw in the Ad Library opens on family seasonal strength and the 113% absorption study, not your page's line: "Vitamin D supplementation is officially recommended for all during autumn and winter." It is dynamic and may rotate other texts; whether leading with that guidance brings cheaper purchases is untested.

One change

In a Meta A/B test, randomly split one UK audience at equal budget between two fixed-copy ads, not dynamic, with the same image, study citation and Vitamin D page: today's opening or the guidance first. Untested draft, pending claims sign-off: "Vitamin D supplementation is officially recommended for all during autumn and winter. Ours is 113% better absorbed than the leading high street tablet. Shop Vitamin D."

02 / Paid ads

Test a bloating-answer creatine ad message

Of the two creatine ads we saw, one opens "Modern life demands more. So should your Creatine." and the other "Need more in the tank?"; both then describe the formula, and the dynamic one may rotate other texts. Your page's FAQ answers bloating and says "a loading phase is optional", but whether leading on them cuts first-purchase cost is untested.

One change

In a Meta A/B test, randomly split one audience at equal budget between two fixed-copy ads, not dynamic, with the same image, claims footnote and Creatine Complex page: today's opening or the FAQ answers first. Untested draft, pending claims sign-off: "Worried about bloating, or think you need a loading phase? Our 5g daily serving is typically well tolerated, and a loading phase is optional."
