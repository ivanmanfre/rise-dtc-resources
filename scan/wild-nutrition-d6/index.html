01 / Paid ads

Add the 20% subscription saving to the creatine ad

The recorded creatine ad's text names no price or offer, so the 20% subscription saving may first appear on the product page. Its image was not seen and may show one. UK visitors see £24.00 on subscription against £30.00 one-time.

One change

Proposal, untested: duplicate the Creatine Complex ad and add one line: 'Subscribe and save 20%: £24.00 instead of £30.00.' Keep the original, with its claim footnote, as the control. Run both in UK-targeted ad sets through Meta's A/B test with an even budget, same audience, dates and unchanged page. Exclude any ads that use separate discount codes.

02 / Paid ads

Test a mind-and-muscle opening against the perimenopause creatine ad

The recorded creatine ad opens with perimenopause and muscles, while the product page frames the formula more widely. Which opening brings more buyers is open to test.

One change

Proposal, untested: duplicate the ad and open the copy with 'Power is more than muscle. Mind and muscle support in one daily serving.' Keep the original as control, with the same claim footnote and destination. Run both through Meta's A/B test with an even budget, same audience and dates. Only the opening angle changes.

03 / Paid ads

Test a small-dose answer in the collagen creator ad

Collagen 500 Plus is third in your best-selling sort with 621 reviews, and its page asks why the dose is so low, so dose looks like a live buying question. The recorded creator-style ad text does not mention dose; the video itself was not reviewed.

One change

Proposal, untested: duplicate the creator-style video ad and add an opening caption: 'Just 3g a day: 500 Dalton peptides. Smaller peptides, smarter formula.' Keep the original as control, with the same page, audience and dates. This is a message test only; check creator usage rights and your claims review first.
