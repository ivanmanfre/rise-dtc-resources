01 / Proposed paid test

Test returnable pouch versus scent-led ad message

Scent has to be chosen without a trial, since your FAQ says samples are not offered at the moment. Your homepage pitches both refillable design and scents developed with Reia nose Abi Titterington-Lough. It is unclear which reason gets a first-time buyer to commit to an unsmelled wash.

One change

Split one audience of people new to Reia at random, with the same budget, and send both ads to your homepage bestsellers. Ad A (refill): "Buy the bottle once. Refills come by post, and the pouch goes back free. Natural hand and body wash, refillable by design." Ad B (scent): "Forest SILVA, green VERDE or woody FEN. Find the scent that feels like your ritual."

What we saw
Scent has to be chosen without a trial, since your FAQ says samples are not offered at the moment. Your homepage pitches both refillable design and scents developed with Reia nose Abi Titterington-Lough. It is unclear which reason gets a first-time buyer to commit to an unsmelled wash.

What to measure
Winner: first-order revenue per pound of ad spend, after discounts. Random audience split, same budget and window, two weeks minimum. Cost per first purchase and subscriptions are context only.
