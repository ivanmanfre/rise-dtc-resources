Finding 1 · Shopper paths

Your best seller is sold out

Shoppers who click through to OCTOPUS TATTOO - WHITE POCKET T-SHIRT ~ BEN WILLS COLLAB reach a Sold out button, so anyone who came for it can't order it today.

Recommended next step

Restock it, or move an in-stock product into its best-seller and homepage spots until it's back.

Best-selling order on your store: Your best seller, marked sold out. See this on your storefront

Finding 2 · Shopper paths

Free shipping starts at £50, and your typical product costs £20

A shopper buying one typical item at £20 pays for delivery unless they add more to reach £50.

Recommended next step

Test a threshold closer to one or two items, or a bundle priced just above £50.

Free-shipping threshold: £50, against a £20 median product price. See this on your storefront

Finding 3 · Shopper paths

5.8% of your variants are sold out while your 5 Meta ads run

You have 5 active ads on Meta, and 5.8% of your variants read out of stock right now. A click that lands on a sold-out option can't turn into an order.

Recommended next step

Pull sold-out products out of your ads and product feed until they're back in stock.

Out of stock right now: 5.8% of your variants. See this on your storefront
