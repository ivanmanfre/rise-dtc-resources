What we saw
Your gift ad says the cover fits the chair they already have, but not how a gift buyer finds the right one. Its landing page lists 161 products with the brand filter unselected, so the buyer must match cover to chair alone.

What to measure
Cost per purchase and revenue per ad click for both ads, with the same audience, budget, page and dates. Also count contacts to customer service about a wrong fit.
