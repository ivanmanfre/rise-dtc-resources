01 / Paid ads

Show the 20% Black Friday saving on each price

Your ad and its landing collection promise 20% off everything, yet sampled cards such as the Nijntje stoelverkleiner show a single price, and the matching-set widget headed 20% totals 254,80 against 290,75, the same item prices already listed. The homepage also shows the Cloud Z cover at 79,95 beside an old price of 65,00.

One change

Today, fix the Cloud Z old price and state on the collection banner and set widget how the 20% is applied. Then test cards and the set widget showing each item's final price, once you confirm how the 20% combines with existing BLACKFRIDAY markdowns, against the current display.

02 / Paid ads

Turn the mess ad into a one-wipe video

Your 'let them mess' ad asks parents to believe a cleaning promise from a still image. The cushion collection it lands on states the cushion is clean in one wipe and that coated and velvet cushions can be wiped with a damp cloth.

One change

Make a short video version of the same ad: real mess on a coated Tripp Trapp stoelverkleiner, one wipe with a damp cloth, the child carrying on eating. Keep caption, audience and destination identical to the image ad.

What we saw
Your 'let them mess' ad asks parents to believe a cleaning promise from a still image. The cushion collection it lands on states the cushion is clean in one wipe and that coated and velvet cushions can be wiped with a damp cloth.

What to measure
Compare purchases and revenue per person reached for image versus video, same audience, destination and window. Also check how many viewers click and the ad cost per purchase.

Test setup
