01 / Proposed paid test

Fix the conflicting return terms, then test guarantee ads

A link across your site promises '30‑day results or it’s free, period' and goes to PDRN cream, and the homepage says '30 Day Free Returns'. Yet the refund policy offers a pre-paid return label, then says customers pay return shipping and must send all original packaging, so settle this before an ad leads with it.

One change

Rewrite the refund policy so it states once who pays return shipping and whether a part-used jar qualifies, and repeat that line under the PDRN guarantee. Then send two new, untested ads to the PDRN page: A 'No results in 30 days? Full refund, terms on page' and B 'Deep hydration with PINK PDRN cream'.

02 / Your store

Make the VitaBoost three-jar set cheaper per jar

Your VitaBoost multi-buy makes the three-jar set a worse deal per jar than the Two Pack. Three jars at $28.76 work out to $9.59 each, against $9.11 each in the Two Pack at $18.22, which is labelled BEST VALUE.

One change

For everyone, fix the 'Tripple' spelling and show each option's per-jar cost under its price. Then show half of visitors a three-jar price of $27.33 or less, matching the Two Pack's $9.11 per jar.

What we saw
Your VitaBoost multi-buy makes the three-jar set a worse deal per jar than the Two Pack. Three jars at $28.76 work out to $9.59 each, against $9.11 each in the Two Pack at $18.22, which is labelled BEST VALUE.

What to measure
Winner: VitaBoost gross margin per visitor, with visitors randomly split over the same dates. Also check: revenue per visitor and share choosing each pack size.

03 / Your store

Offer VitaBoost cream as a wand add-on

Your product pages say the formulas are paired with the wand to boost absorption, and a review describes using the wand with the capsule cream. The wand buying area we saw shows colour and quantity choices, with no cream option beside them, though your upsell apps may already offer cream in the cart.

One change

For half of wand page visitors, add a tick box under the colour choice that adds one VitaBoost Capsule Cream jar at its current single-jar price.
