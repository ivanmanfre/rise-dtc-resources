01

Primer page: add a two-step Bonder then Primer block with an add-both button against the current text-only pairing

Your Primer page states it is used as step 2 after the Bonder, and your Bonder page states it is used with the Primer. In the pages sampled, the pairing is explained in text only, so a visual two-step route is a distinct test.

Proposed test

Control: current Primer page. Variant: add a 'Bonder then Primer' block under the product details with one add-both button and this draft line: 'Steg 1 Bonder, steg 2 Primer. Lägg till båda för prepp som håller.' Split Primer page traffic 50/50. Only the pairing block changes.

How we’d measure it

Revenue per Primer page visitor in both arms. Guardrails: Primer-only purchase rate and return requests.

02

Builder Gel RYAN page: add a first-time handling and flash cure note beside Add to cart

The RYAN page states a cure time in UV. Review replies from the store explain that lamp heat is normal and suggest flash curing, so that guidance currently sits inside the review replies rather than near the buying decision.

Proposed test

Control: current RYAN page. Variant: add a short first-time note beside Add to cart: 'Rör inte om gelen. Blir det varmt i lampan? Flash cura: härda 1-2 sekunder, ta ut handen, upprepa.' Split traffic 50/50. Price, images and reviews stay unchanged.

How we’d measure it

Completed RYAN purchases per product page visitor in both arms. Guardrails: return requests and review comments about lamp heat.

03

Swatch ad route: gel collection page versus the single Fiber Gel OSCAR page

Your ad text says it swatches all fiber gel colours, but its recorded destination is the single Fiber Gel OSCAR page. Your catalogue also lists Fiber Gel OLIVER at 359 SEK as a separate product, so a collection route is a distinct option.

Proposed test

Keep the ad creative identical, with equal budget, audience and dates. Control: the Fiber Gel OSCAR product page. Variant: the gel collection page. Hypothesis: shoppers who want to compare colours can pick a shade faster from a collection than from one product page.

How we’d measure it

Winning metric for both arms: purchase revenue per ad click. Diagnostics: product page views and add to cart. Guardrails: average order value and return requests.

04

Cart add-on row: Nagellim and MULTI-MIST framed around the 999 kr free shipping line

Your site states free shipping over 999 kr within Sweden. Your best-selling list includes low-priced consumables such as Nagellim at 89 SEK and MULTI-MIST, Prep & Clean at 119 SEK, which could work as small top-ups for baskets near that line.

Proposed test

Control: current cart. Variant: for baskets where adding one or both products would take the order above 999 kr, show an add-on row with Nagellim and MULTI-MIST and this draft line: 'Fyll på med förbrukning och nå fri frakt.' Split cart sessions 50/50. Only the add-on row changes; no discount is added.

How we’d measure it

Revenue per cart session in both arms. Guardrails: purchase completion rate and return requests.

Finding 1 · Repeat purchase

No subscribe or refill option on Builder Gel PHILIP

Builder Gel PHILIP is something a customer uses up, but its page only sells one-time orders, so the next order depends on them remembering to come back.

Recommended next step

Test a subscribe-and-save option on Builder Gel PHILIP, with a small discount on each repeat delivery.
