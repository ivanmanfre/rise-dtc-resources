01 / Paid ads

The 971 Scarf: Test UAE Craftsmanship Against Three Included Sticker Sheets

The 971 Scarf carousel opens with a tribute to the UAE, then mentions the three included sticker sheets before describing local craftsmanship. The product page repeats both selling points and lists fulfilment by 27 November; the test asks which point should lead the ad.

One change

Run only if the confirmed pre-order window and available stock allow a fair comparison. Change just the carousel opener: “Pre-order The 971 Scarf: designed and made in the UAE” versus “Pre-order The 971 Scarf: three sticker sheets included”; keep the remaining copy, images and product page the same.

02 / Proposed paid test

Test Sticker Application Against Sheet Closeups

The open question is whether seeing the No Place Like Home stickers in use sells more sheets than seeing the artwork alone. An anonymous reviewer on the product page says the stickers look good on their laptop and phone.

One change

Make two short Meta videos of the same length: one peels and applies the stickers to a laptop and phone, inspired by that review; the other shows closeups of the sheet alone. Use the same line, “A little piece of the UAE, wherever you go,” and the same product page for both.

Full test plan & sources +

What we saw
The open question is whether seeing the No Place Like Home stickers in use sells more sheets than seeing the artwork alone. An anonymous reviewer on the product page says the stickers look good on their laptop and phone.

What to measure
Randomly split eligible viewers. Compare paid No Place Like Home sheets per 1,000 people reached over the same window, excluding gifts. Check first-order margin after ad costs.

Test setup

03 / Your store

Match Published Shipping Rates to Checkout

The two shipping pages list different standard rates for both the UAE and GCC. The FAQ also lists a UAE free-delivery threshold, so the correct charge needs to be verified before it goes into an ad.

One change

Verify what checkout charges by destination and basket, then align all three pages. Use “Standard delivery to [destination]: [verified charge]” and state any free-delivery rule exactly as it applies at checkout.
