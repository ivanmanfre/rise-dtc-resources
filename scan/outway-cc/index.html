Finding 1 · Shopper paths

6.6% of your products are sold out while your 281 Meta ads run

You have 281 active ads on Meta, and 6.6% of your products read out of stock right now. A click that lands on a sold-out option can't turn into an order.

Finding 2 · Shopper paths

No express checkout button on Mystery Pair

A shopper on Mystery Pair has to add to cart, open the cart and start checkout before they can pay. There's no one-tap button for shoppers who saved their card or use a phone wallet.

Recommended next step

Turn on dynamic checkout buttons in your theme's product page settings.
