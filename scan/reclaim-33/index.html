01 / Proposed paid test

Test three-ways-to-wear ad against saddle-story ad for Campos

Your €580 Campos belt bag page gives two separate reasons to buy: shoppers asked for a bag worn several ways, and it is built from reclaimed saddle leather. Which reason should lead a paid ad is still open, and since each bag is one of a kind, a test gives a rough steer, not a final answer.

One change

Run two new Meta ads to the Campos belt bag collection using the same bag. Ad A films it worn crossbody, at the waist and on the shoulder: 'One bag, three ways. One of a kind, €580.' Ad B films it beside an old saddle: 'Once a saddle. Now your belt bag. One of a kind, €580.'

02 / Your store

Update the hoodie presale date that has passed

The hoodie page still calls itself a presale and says it ships on September 22nd 2025, a date that has passed. A shopper cannot tell whether a €158 order ships now or joins a new production run.

One change

Replace the presale note with the real current status: 'Ready to ship now' if stock exists, or a new dated production run if not. Keep price and sizes unchanged.

03 / Your store

Test a free return collection line on Orient Tote pages

An €880 one-off tote is bought without handling it, so how easy it is to send back is part of the buying decision. Your returns policy offers a free collection service, while the tote FAQ mentions only the 14-day window.

One change

For visitors in countries the free collection covers, add one line under the price: 'Free return collection: request it within 14 days of delivery.'

Compare the two store pages
Full test plan & sources +

What we saw
An €880 one-off tote is bought without handling it, so how easy it is to send back is part of the buying decision. Your returns policy offers a free collection service, while the tote FAQ mentions only the 14-day window.

What to measure
Confirm covered countries, then randomly split their visitors. Compare both versions on purchases and revenue per visitor. Check returns and collection cost after the 14-day request and 14-day return periods.

Test setup
For visitors in countries the free collection covers, add one line under the price: 'Free return collection: request it within 14 days of delivery.'
