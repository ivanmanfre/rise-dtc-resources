01 / Paid ads

Name the real plan extras in the plan ad

The plan ad promises 3 free gifts but names peppermint oil, a root cleanser and fewer wash days, which read as product features rather than gifts. The page it sends buyers to lists its 3 free extras as two travel tins and 15% off every refill.

One change

Run a version of this ad that changes only the gifts sentence, keeping the same video and destination. Draft: "Plus 2 free travel tins and 15% off every refill on your first subscription."

02 / Paid ads

Make the refund promise match the policy steps

Your pages describe claiming the 28 day refund in two ways. The grease relief set page, where the ads we read send buyers with that promise, says no forms, while your policy page says customers will be asked to fill in a refund form.

One change

Decide which process you actually run, then make the set page line and the policy say the same thing. If the form stays, draft: "Love it or we refund you, and you keep the bars. Message us and fill in one refund form, nothing to post back."

Shoppers may meet two cost-per-wash figures. The cost-per-wash ads we read quote 18p and send buyers to the grease relief set page, which states 24p a wash for up to 80 washes, and the page text we read does not show how 18p is reached.

One change

Run a copy of the cost-per-wash ad that uses the page's own figure, with the same image and destination. Draft: "Up to 80 washes, about 24p a wash on the grease relief plan."

06 / Your store

Explain bar lifespan beside the subscription delivery gap

Subscribers on the peppermint set page must pick a delivery gap of 6, 8 or 10 weeks. Your FAQ says a bar lasts about 8 to 12 weeks depending on wash frequency, and subscribers can already pause or change. Showing that lifespan at the choice may help buyers start on a gap that suits them.

One change

Add one line under the delivery choice. Draft: "Each bar usually lasts 8 to 12 weeks, depending on how often you wash. Pick the gap that fits and change it anytime."

Full test plan & sources +

What we saw
Subscribers on the peppermint set page must pick a delivery gap of 6, 8 or 10 weeks. Your FAQ says a bar lasts about 8 to 12 weeks depending on wash frequency, and subscribers can already pause or change. Showing that lifespan at the choice may help buyers start on a gap that suits them.

What to measure
Split visitors 50/50 over the same window. Winner: revenue per visitor over at least 12 weeks, counting repeat deliveries. Subscription starts, skips, pauses and cancellations are side checks.
