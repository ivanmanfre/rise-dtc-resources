What we saw
The founder-story ad ends with "Discover the future of skincare!", while your homepage promises "skincare designed for real, reactive skin". Of the twelve ad texts we read, only two mention sensitive skin or being fragrance free, so the founder ad could lead with that promise.

What to measure
Purchases and revenue per 1,000 ad impressions for both versions in a Meta A/B test, same audience, budget and dates. Click rate is a diagnostic only.

Test setup
Run a version of the founder-story ad we read (Ad Library ID 1084482090885016) with the same image and homepage destination. Add one new first line so the promise shows first, and keep the rest unchanged. Draft: "Built for real, reactive skin. Fragrance-free self-tan skincare for a natural glow."
