01 / Proposed paid test

Test taste-led versus mental-fatigue ads for greens powder

Active Green Pro's Vitality & Brain Greens page offers both a taste promise and a mental-fatigue support claim, plus a Health Canada licence and a made in Canada claim. We found no active Meta or Google ads, so a first ad test can show which reason sells this one product, not your whole range.

One change

Proposed new Meta ad test, not an observed ad. Both variants target the same Ontario audience, where orders of $79.99+ ship free, and send to the same Vitality & Brain Greens product page. Variant A (taste): "One scoop. Pineapple-coconut greens that don't taste like grass." Variant B (support): "Used in herbal medicine to help reduce mental fatigue in cases of mental stress. One scoop daily. Read label and cautions."

02 / Your store

Test a two-bottle krill oil supply option

Your krill oil page states that 60 softgels last 15 days, so one bottle covers about two weeks. The page text we read shows only a quantity box (we did not check your Bundle and save deals), and one bottle sits "$36.00 away from free shipping" in Ontario, while two ($87.98) clear the $79.99 threshold.

One change

On the krill oil page, add a selectable '30-day supply: 2 bottles' option beside the quantity box. Draft line: 'Two bottles cover 30 days of the listed directions. Ontario orders over $79.99 ship free.' The current page stays as it is for comparison.

03 / Your store

Test complementary items above greens alternatives on the greens page

The first related row on the greens powder page, "Pairs well with", lists other greens and energy powders. Complementary items such as an L-theanine capsule sit lower under "You may also like". The page also shows how far the cart is "away from free shipping".

One change

On the Vitality & Brain greens page, show the complementary items the store already lists lower down, such as the L-theanine product, in the first related row instead of the other greens. Draft heading: "Round out your daily routine". Leave out Magnesium Sleep Support, given the label caution on products that cause drowsiness, and check the L-theanine label for any drowsiness caution first. The current page stays unchanged.
