Finding 1 · Shopper paths

No express checkout button on Peachy Scrunch Leggings

A shopper on Peachy Scrunch Leggings has to add to cart, open the cart and start checkout before they can pay. There's no one-tap button for shoppers who saved their card or use a phone wallet.
