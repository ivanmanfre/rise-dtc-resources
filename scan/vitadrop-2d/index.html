01 / Proposed paid test

Test Skin vs Workout-and-Travel Ads for the Inside Out Hydration Bundle

Your Inside Out Hydration Bundle sells hydration with a skincare promise, while a buyer review of Rapid Hydration Sugar-Free describes using it for workouts and travel. We found no ad showing which angle sells better, and with the Sugar-Free 30Pk sold out when we read it, this in-stock bundle, which includes it, is one page to test both on.

One change

Run two ads to the Inside Out Hydration Bundle page, both as a written line on the same image. A: 'Your skin is the last place your body sends water. Hydrate from the inside.' B: 'Sugar-free hydration for workouts and travel.' The page leads with skin, so read a B loss as a result for this page only, not a verdict on the angle.

02 / Proposed paid test

Test Fast-Relief vs 21-Day Routine Gut Relief Ads

The Gut Relief page makes two separate promises, quick relief and longer-term benefit, and no recorded ad shows which one should lead. Your page states most users notice relief within 30 minutes to 2 hours, and that benefits build with daily use over 21+ days.

One change

Run two ads to the Gut Relief page. A: 'Bloated? Most users notice relief within 30 minutes to 2 hours.' B: 'A daily sachet for your gut, with benefits that build over 21+ days.' Check both against Australian therapeutic goods advertising rules first.

What we saw
The Gut Relief page makes two separate promises, quick relief and longer-term benefit, and no recorded ad shows which one should lead. Your page states most users notice relief within 30 minutes to 2 hours, and that benefits build with daily use over 21+ days.

What to measure
Meta split test: random audience halves, same budget and window. Winner: revenue per dollar spent. Later: subscription revenue per buyer over 12 weeks, across both plans. Also track refunds.

03 / Proposed paid test

Send Paid Traffic to Try Me Pack vs Best Sellers

Paid visitors need a first page to land on, and no ad journey was recorded. Your Try Me Pack offers four formulas in 7-serve packs for $40, while the Best Sellers page we read marks three of its four full-size packs Sold Out and does not list the Try Me Pack.

One change

Run one identical ad and randomly split its visitors between the Try Me Pack page and the Best Sellers collection. Draft: 'Not sure which Vitadrop suits you? Find the formula that fits your routine.'

Paid visitors need a first page to land on, and no ad journey was recorded. Your Try Me Pack offers four formulas in 7-serve packs for $40, while the Best Sellers page we read marks three of its four full-size packs Sold Out and does not list the Try Me Pack.
