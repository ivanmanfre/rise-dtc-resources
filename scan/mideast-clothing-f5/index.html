01 / Proposed paid test

Test a sale-price ad versus a color-choice ad for the Jewish Star Sun Faded Hoodie

Your top item in the best-selling sort, the Jewish Star Sun Faded Hoodie, shows $80 beside a struck-through $110 compare-at price and comes in Gray, Blue and Rose Pink. Two reviews praise the colors, "Loved loved loved the pink version!!!" and "AMAZING COLOR SCHEME", so color may rival price as the reason to buy.

One change

Proposed new ad test, not a live ad. Run two Meta ads to the same Jewish Star Sun Faded Hoodie page. Ad A draft: "The Jewish Star Sun Faded Hoodie. $80. See it here." Ad B draft: "One hoodie, three shades. Gray, Blue or Rose Pink. Pick yours." Hold the sale price. The page says "Only 10 Units Created", so confirm stock first.

03 / Your store

Clarify sale-item return terms beside the size picker

The Jewish Star Sun Faded Hoodie is listed at a sale price across five sizes. Your refund policy states sale items cannot be returned, and the sampled page text does not repeat that rule near the sizes. A shopper unsure of fit has to find it elsewhere.

One change

Your shipping and refund pages word the sale rule differently, so first make them match. Then show half of visitors on that product page a short note under the size picker. Draft, to finalise once both policies match: 'Sale item: please confirm your size before ordering. Sale items cannot be returned, except EU orders within 14 days.'

04 / Your store

Use one sale-item return rule on both policies

Your shipping page says final sale items cannot be returned, while your refund page says sale items cannot be returned, and EU orders get a 14-day return right. Both pages say you offer store credit instead of refunds, yet the refund page also describes refunds to the original payment method, so shoppers cannot tell which rule applies.

One change

Pick one rule and use the same sentence on both policy pages, naming which marked-down items are final sale, stating the EU 14-day exception and saying once whether returns earn refunds or store credit. Do this before adding any return note to product pages.
