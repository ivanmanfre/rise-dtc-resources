01 / Paid ads

Test age-30 problem versus 30-day result ad message

One sampled ad opens with skin that stops bouncing back around age 30. Two others lead with younger-looking skin in 30 days. All send people to the Men's Skincare Set page, which also talks about 30 days.

One changeRun two ads with the same audience, budget, image and destination (the Men's Skincare Set page). Ad A opens: "Around 30, skin stops bouncing back. Scrub, Serum, Moisturizer: 3 simple steps." Ad B opens: "Want younger-looking skin in 30 days? Scrub, Serum, Moisturizer: 3 simple steps." Both end with the 30-day money-back guarantee.

Purchases and first-order revenue per ad click on the set page, same window for both ads. Guardrail: cost per purchase and refund requests. Run until both ads have enough purchases to compare.

02 / Your store

Match subscription copy to the 6-week supply

Your set page states a set will last around 4-6 weeks, and the purchase options offer delivery every 6 weeks. The timing already fits, so a short line linking the two could be worth testing.

One changeAdd one line beside the delivery choice: "One set lasts about 6 weeks, so a delivery every 6 weeks arrives as you run low. Skip or pause anytime." Run it against the current page and change nothing else.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
Completed first orders per visitor in both arms, with 30-day refund requests as a guardrail. Track second deliveries kept after 6 weeks separately, over at least 12 weeks.

03 / Your store

Add a routine panel to single product pages

Your moisturizer page tells customers to apply the serum first, and your serum page describes fine lines, uneven tone and dryness in one bottle. Both pages already explain the routine, so a clearer way to add the other steps could be tested.

One changeOn the moisturizer and serum pages, add a small panel under Add to cart. Draft: "Works best as scrub, serum, moisturizer. Add this step, or start the full routine." Compare with the current pages.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
Purchase revenue per visitor on these two pages in both arms. Guardrails: average order value and 30-day refund requests.
