01 / Paid ads

Test a three-minute routine ad hook

Your ad opens on skin that stops bouncing back around 30, then sends people to the men's skincare set page. That page states a routine that takes under 3 minutes, twice a day, a timing the sampled ad text does not mention.

One change

Keep the same destination and run one new ad against the current one, changing only the opening line. Draft: "Three steps. Under three minutes. Skin that looks like you slept eight hours." Keep the image, audience and budget split the same, with each ad in its own ad set. This is a message test only.

02 / Paid ads

Test a guarantee-led ad hook

Your ad leads with an outcome question about younger-looking skin. The page it lands on also states a 30-day money-back guarantee, which could be tested as the lead idea instead of the result.

One change

Keep the destination and add a second ad led by the trial instead of the result. Draft: "Try the 3-step routine for 30 days. Not happy? We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee." Keep the image, audience and budget split the same, with each ad in its own ad set. This is a message test only.

03 / Paid ads

Test a four-week forehead claim in the ad

Your ad says "Say goodbye to fine lines and hello to radiant skin" in 30 days. Your page states that most men report a narrower result within 4 weeks, which could be tested in the ad.

One change

Keep the destination and run an ad using the page's own wording against the current one. Draft: "Smoother forehead, softer lines around the eyes. Most men report it within 4 weeks." Use equal budget and audience, with each ad in its own ad set. This is a message test only.
