01 / Paid ads

Test daily-use copy against the three-week hook

Your ad uses a three-week wellbeing promise. Its actual landing page distinguishes early improvement from full effect, so these timelines are not inherently contradictory; a daily-routine hook offers a different message to compare.

From your public ad

“in nur 3 Wochen endlich wieder wohl in seiner Haut”
View the actual ad ↗
One changeReplace only the ad sentence promising a change in three weeks with: "Kratzen und Pfotenlecken? Entdecke Bauchbalance als tägliche Futterroutine." Keep the same creative, remaining copy and recorded landing page; compare both messages with a random audience split.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
Compare purchases and first-order revenue per ad click; monitor acquisition cost. Recheck refunds after the 60-day guarantee and contribution after ad spend before choosing a winner.

02 / Your store

Show the full two-bag price upfront

In the Bauchbalance selector, one-time purchase and Sparabo already sit together. With 10–15 kg and the two-bag Sparabo selected, the card shows 32,26 € while the buying button shows 64,52 €; explicitly labelling the per-bag price and total is worth testing.

One changeOn that two-bag option, add: "32,26 € pro Beutel · 64,52 € für 2 Beutel". Keep the current prices, selection, delivery interval and gifts; compare against the existing card.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
Compare first-order revenue per product-page visitor. Track selector-to-purchase completion and refunds through the 60-day guarantee; check contribution after discounts, goods and fulfilment costs before scaling.

03 / Your store

Replace related toppings with one Schleckmatte routine add-on

The sampled Bauchbalance page closes with a row of other toppings under a generic related-products heading. The Schleckmatte page describes the mat as part of the daily topping routine, so a single complementary add-on is worth comparing against that row.

One changeOn the Bauchbalance page, replace the related-toppings row with one Schleckmatte card. Draft copy: "Topping drauf, Schleckmatte dazu: die tägliche Routine wird ein ruhiger Moment." Control keeps the current row.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
Winning metric: purchase revenue per visitor on the Bauchbalance page. Diagnostic: share of orders including the Schleckmatte. Guardrail: Bauchbalance topping purchase rate.
