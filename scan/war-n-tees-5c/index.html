01 / Proposed paid test

Test combat photo story against Vet pledge ad message

No active Meta or Google ads were found, so a first paid test could compare two openings: your homepage's favorite combat photos or its Vet pledge. Your FAQ ties the 21% for apparel to net profits, while digital products use gross sales, so tee ad wording should say net profits.

One change

Proposed new Meta ad test. Use Meta's A/B test so both ads get equal budget, audience and image style, and link to the same homepage. Ad A: "Wear a combat photo with a story.
