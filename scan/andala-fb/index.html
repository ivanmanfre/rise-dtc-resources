01 / Paid ads

Tell product-ad clickers the store opens with a password on closed sale days

The DeBloat, GlucoGuard and Cortisol Calm product ads we read, like the "No, no todo es SIBO" video, link to product-specific pages. On 26 November each showed the password page, naming no product or 10% offer (a 4 August 2025 archive copy shows DeBloat's with add-to-cart). Your private-sale ads invite people to join; no recorded product-ad text names the gate.

One change

Untested proposal for your next closed sale day: run two copies of the DeBloat sachet ad with equal budget, audience and link. Copy A stays as it is. Copy B adds one line before the offer: "Hoy la web abre solo con contraseña: déjanos tu email y entra." Change nothing else, and first confirm the 10% applies during the sale.

02 / Paid ads

Add the 10% first-order line to the cortisol-wake ad

The recorded text of your Cortisol Calm ad about waking tired after eight hours of sleep has no first-order offer (other text options of this dynamic ad may differ). Your bedtime-thoughts Cortisol Calm ad, linking to the same page, ends with "–10% en tu primera compra". Which converts better is unknown; sleep effects are the ads' own claims.

One change

Untested proposal: run two copies of the cortisol-wake ad with equal budget, audience and placements, both linking to the recorded Cortisol Calm page. Copy B adds one closing line to every text option: "–10% en tu primera compra." Run it once Black Friday offers have ended, since on 26 November that link opened the password page.

03 / Paid ads

Test the GlucoGuard period-cravings video against the afternoon-slump video

Two of your GlucoGuard videos open with different personal stories and link to the same GlucoGuard page: one about afternoon energy dips and sugar cravings, one about cravings before a period. Only the afternoon video's recorded text ends with the 10% first-order offer. Which video brings more first-order buyers is unknown; the effects described are the ads' own claims.

One change

Untested proposal: give the period-cravings video the same closing line as the afternoon video, "–10% en tu primera compra.", then run the two videos with equal budgets, audiences and placements to the recorded GlucoGuard page. The whole videos still differ, so credit any winner to the whole video. Run it once Black Friday offers have ended.

One change

Untested proposal: run the lunch-story ad and the sachet ad with equal budgets, audiences and placements, both linking to the recorded DeBloat page with the same 10% offer. Change only the ad, and run it once Black Friday offers have ended so sale offers do not mix in.
