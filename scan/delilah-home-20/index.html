01 / Proposed paid test

Sell the last hemp stock with a 'too rough' to 'softening up' ad

Your hemp page says FINAL PRODUCTION and All Sales Final, so buyers cannot return sheets that feel wrong. Elinor D. says hemp and linen sets often feel crispy on arrival but found this set soft, and Roger K.'s wife first called them too rough, then said they were softening up after a couple of nights.

One change

If the stock left justifies it, and with Roger K.'s permission, film a short dramatized scene from his review. Draft caption: 'Night 1: too rough. A couple of nights later: I think they're softening up. Dramatized from a real hemp sheet review. Final production, all sales final.' Run it against a hemp product-shot ad with the same audience, budget and hemp page destination.

02 / Your store

Label homepage tiles with the set price shoppers land on

Your homepage shows the sheet collection at a bare $39.99, but the sheet page opens on a Twin XL set at $199.99. Towels follow the same pattern: listed from $24.99, then opening on a 6 piece set at $124.99.

One change

Relabel the homepage sheet and towel tiles with the set price each page opens on, for example 'Twin XL sheet set $199.99' and '6 piece towel set $124.99', with the lower price shown as 'smaller items from'.

03 / Your store

04 / Your store

Clarify bundle contents and pitch the 12 pack

Your Bundle Sets page says it includes 4 six-piece sets, but also lists the 12 pack as 4 face, 4 hand and 4 bath towels, which matches two 6 piece sets. Two sets at $124.99 cost more than the pack at $239.99, but the page does not say so.

One change

Correct the contents line today. Then, only for colors where the 12 pack is sold at $239.99, test one line, matched to live prices, on the 6 piece set page: 'Need two sets? The 12 Assorted Pack is $239.99, $9.99 less than buying two.'

05 / Your store

State one free shipping rule across banner and pages

Your banner says free freight runs until November 30th, while the shop page lists Free shipping with no end date. Shoppers cannot tell whether free shipping is a holiday deal or standard, which matters before promoting any offer.

One change

Pick one rule and show it the same way on the banner, shop page and product pages: either 'Free shipping on every order' or 'Free freight ends Nov 30'.
