01 / Paid ads

Test the toothbrush page as the landing page for your recycled toothbrush ads

Your two recycled-handle videos send clicks to the homepage, which opens on Grin Pro toothpastes and shows kids brushes and one Pro toothbrush duo, none named as recycled, plus a general recycled-plastic note. The adult toothbrushes page lists your 100% Recycled brushes by name, in single packs and 4-packs.

One change

Split clicks from the two recycled-handle videos evenly between the homepage and the adult toothbrushes page, with the video and caption unchanged. This is a route test only, not a message test.

02 / Proposed paid test

Test a morning coffee scene in a whitening ad

Your Enzyme Whitening page answers a specific reason to buy: it states the toothpaste is designed to help remove everyday surface stains, including coffee and tea stains. That everyday moment is concrete enough to film and could make the whitening promise easier to picture.

One change

Make two new videos sent to the Enzyme Whitening 113g page, identical except the opening. Version A opens on a morning coffee, then brushing, with text: 'Coffee every day? Made for everyday surface stains.' Version B opens with a general whitening line. Shared caption draft: 'Designed to help remove coffee and tea surface stains. It targets surface stains, not your natural tooth colour.'

Full test plan & sources +

What we saw
Your Enzyme Whitening page answers a specific reason to buy: it states the toothpaste is designed to help remove everyday surface stains, including coffee and tea stains. That everyday moment is concrete enough to film and could make the whitening promise easier to picture.

What to measure
Winning metric: first-order revenue per 1,000 impressions, both openings in one Meta A/B test, equal budget, audience, page and dates. Diagnostics: click and purchase rate. Confirm margins on the call.

Made for everyday surface stains.' Version B opens with a general whitening line. Shared caption draft: 'Designed to help remove coffee and tea surface stains. It targets surface stains, not your natural tooth colour.'

03 / Your store

Add pause or cancel wording to the subscribe option

Your homepage sells subscriptions with 'Pause or cancel anytime'. On the Microbiome page as we read it, the subscribe option shows $10.40 but not those terms, while a 25% sale headlines $9.75, below that price.

One change

Once the 25% sale ends, add 'Pause or cancel anytime' directly under the subscribe option for half of Microbiome page visitors. Change nothing else.

04 / Your store

Fix the $13.00 pack label on the travel tube

On the 20g Enzyme Whitening travel tube page as served, the price shows $3.99 but the pack picker reads $13.00 per pack, the 113g full-size price. The page invites shoppers to try the product before buying the full size, so a clear price matters at that first step.

One change

If the live page also shows $13.00/pack, correct the 1-pack label to the $3.99 tube price and show the real 6-pack per-tube price as charged in the cart.

Full test plan & sources +

What we saw
On the 20g Enzyme Whitening travel tube page as served, the price shows $3.99 but the pack picker reads $13.00 per pack, the 113g full-size price. The page invites shoppers to try the product before buying the full size, so a clear price matters at that first step.

What to measure
This is a fix, not a split test. Confirm the shown labels match cart prices for 1 and 6 packs. Track purchase rate before and after as background only.

Test setup
If the live page also shows $13.00/pack, correct the 1-pack label to the $3.99 tube price and show the real 6-pack per-tube price as charged in the cart.
