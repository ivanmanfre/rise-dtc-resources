Group 01

Research

What competitors already claim, what customers already say in their own words, and which sales angles nothing you're running touches. Run this group before anyone writes a line of new copy.

01 to 03

01Start here The Competitor Read Paste ad listings and page text for a few competitors, plus your own product facts. Get their claims quoted word for word, their offer set beside yours, and the angle nobody in the space is running. competitor-researcher
02 The Customer Language Bank Paste reviews, survey answers, tickets, or return reasons in bulk. Get a language bank of benefits, objections, and before and after states, plus ad-ready phrases pulled straight from your customers' own words. customer-researcher
03 The Angle Gap Map Paste the name and primary text of your live ads. Get each ad's angle counted, plus the angles on a fixed list that none of your ads are running. angle-gap-mapper

Group 02

Creative

Turning one angle into a brief, a hook set, a script, or the next round of tests, each one bound to facts you actually supplied.

04 to 07

04 The Ad Brief Paste one angle, the product facts you can stand behind, and the format you're shooting. Get a shot-by-shot brief with a claim boundary block naming exactly what those facts let you say. ad-brief-generator
05 The Hook Set Paste one angle, your product facts, and the audience in a sentence. Get a set of opening lines, each tagged with the device it uses and the fact it rests on. hook-generator
06 The Next Six Paste the copy of your best current ad, a line on its visual, and what you already tried. Get the next round of variants, each changing one named element, ordered cheapest to shoot first. winner-iteration-planner
07 The UGC Script Paste one angle, your product facts, a line about the creator, and a target length. Get a beat-by-beat script where every beat carries its spoken line, its on-screen action, its overlay text, and its timing. ugc-script-writer

Group 03

Media buying

Whether your tracking links survive into your reports, whether the account structure itself is clean, and how far you can safely push a budget.

08 to 10

08 The UTM Tag Audit Paste the destination link behind every live ad. Get a pass or break status per link naming the exact defect, with the corrected link printed. utm-tag-auditor
09 The Account Hygiene Audit Paste the campaign and ad set list as it reads on your Ads Manager screen. Get a fixed hygiene verdict across every check, the wasted spend summed from your own rows, and a fix list naming the exact campaign or ad set. ad-account-hygiene-auditor
10 The Budget Step Planner Paste one campaign's current daily budget, its recent weekly and monthly rows, and the cost per purchase the brand can afford. Get the next daily budget, how long to hold it, and the condition that puts it back. budget-step-planner

Group 04

Conversion

What the ad promises against what the page actually says, checked line by line. Every quoted line comes back verbatim from what you pasted.

11 to 12

11 The Message Match Checker Paste the ad's primary text and headline, plus the page copy down to the first buy button. Get every promise classed answered, partial, or unanswered, with the page line that answers it quoted verbatim. message-match-checker
12 The Page Checklist Paste the page text top to bottom, the ad sending traffic to it, and the device split. Get a pass, fail, or cannot-check table down the page, with the page line quoted and one fix per fail. cro-auditor

Group 05

The money

The arithmetic under the account: what a purchase can cost you, whether the platforms and your backend agree, and the call on every live ad.

13 to 15

13 Break-Even and Affordable CPA Paste the order-economics lines off a Shopify order screen, a supplier invoice, and a processor statement. Get contribution margin, break-even ROAS, break-even CPA, the same figures after returns, and the CPA you can afford at a contribution you name. break-even-and-affordable-cpa
14 Platform ROAS Versus MER Paste per-channel spend and reported revenue, each platform's attribution setting, and your backend revenue for the same window. Get per-channel ROAS, MER on the backend figure, and the gap between them in dollars and as a percent of backend revenue. roas-vs-mer-reader
15 The Scale-Or-Kill Judge Paste the per-ad spend and purchase rows you can read off Ads Manager for one window. Get scale, hold, cut, or not-enough-spend for every ad, never a guess where the row is too thin to read. scale-or-kill-judge

How to install

You need a Claude account and the folder

There is nothing to connect and no setup step that needs anyone technical. Both routes below work. Pick whichever matches how you already use Claude.

Step 01 · Download and unzip

Get the folder, then open one skill in Claude

Start by downloading the kit and unzipping it. Inside are fifteen skill folders, each holding one SKILL.md and a fixtures/ folder of practice input. Every route below reads off that unzipped folder.

›Download all 15 skills

Route 1: Claude Code

  1. Put the unzipped dtc-claude-skills folder inside the project you work in.
  2. Run: cp -R dtc-claude-skills/* ~/.claude/skills/
  3. Invoke a skill by name, for example run scale-or-kill-judge on this, then paste your input underneath.

One command copies all fifteen folders into place. Claude Code picks them up on the next run.

Route 2: any Claude chat

  1. Open the skill's folder and its SKILL.md file.
  2. Copy the whole file and paste it into a new chat.
  3. Paste your own numbers or copy underneath it and send.

No setup at all. This works in Claude on the web, in the desktop app, and on mobile, and it is where to start if you only want to try one skill first.

The folder, and how it is laid out

Worth knowing before you open it

What these will not do

Every one of these files works the same way, off the same rule. It reads what you paste, it prints what that supports, and it stops rather than guess.

  • No ad account or store connection

    Nothing here logs into Meta, Google, TikTok, or Shopify. There is no key to enter and no permission to grant, because there is nothing for a skill to connect to.

  • Only what you paste

    Every skill reads only the input sitting under it in the chat. It never reaches past that paste for a benchmark, an assumption, or a fact from anywhere else.

  • The arithmetic is shown

    Every number a skill prints is computed from a number you gave it, and the working that got there is printed too, so you can redo it by hand and check it.

  • Missing input, named

    When a skill needs a figure that was not in your paste, it prints [NEEDS:] and names the exact field, instead of filling the gap with a plausible guess.
