Two places paid ads can grow REBL Scents

Finding 1

Both your live Meta ads link to a Valentino bottle, and neither links to your own Vanilla Muse

Your two live Meta ads both link to Valentino Born in Roma, where the 3.4 oz and the gift set are sold out and the in-stock sizes cost $104.98 and $129.98. Neither ad links to Vanilla Muse, which your page describes as a REBL Scents exclusive: 3 oz for $69.95, with a caramel and vanilla base, in stock.

What we'd test against itREBL Vanilla Muse EDP3.0 oz $69.95 · Made in USAIn stock · "REBL Scents Exclusive" on its page

What we saw

Ad "For the vanilla lovers" and ad "Smell like a snack all summer long", both live since 12 July, both link to the Valentino page.

Why it matters

Shoppers can price a Valentino bottle at other retailers, and discount stores advertise designer scents at up to 80% off. A scent your page calls exclusive is harder to price-shop. Whether that sells better is what the test answers.

The change

Make a Vanilla Muse version of the vanilla ad and run it beside the current one. The second ad's hashtags name Valentino, so the existing video can only be reused if it doesn't show the Valentino bottle.

Proposed paid test · not yet run

Current Valentino ad against a Vanilla Muse ad. Version A: the current ad and Valentino page. Version B: Vanilla Muse creative (draft below) linking to the Vanilla Muse page. Same audience, budget and dates.

This tests the product, the creative and the page together. Winner: purchase revenue per dollar spent. Your margins on each decide what the result is worth; that's what the call is for.

Other things we checked

Before adding catalog ads: 10 of your top 20 best sellers are sold out in every size

You likely know your stock. It matters for ads in one way: catalog ads pull products from your feed, so sold-out items need to be excluded from any feed you advertise from. You don't run catalog ads today, so this is a launch check.

Source: best-selling page and product records, 5 Nov 2026

Your REBL Bundle page says "full sized" but lists 2 ml samples

The description reads "Create your own two fragrance bundle with our full sized REBL Exclusives", then lists each scent as "Sample 2ml / 0.07 fl oz". Fix the size wording before the bundle goes into any ad.

Source: REBL Bundle page, 5 Nov 2026

Google's archive shows no REBL ad after 2 May 2026

Google's public archive lists 13 REBL ads (7 text, 5 image, 1 video), first seen from 20 February 2024. The latest last-shown date is 2 May 2026. The archive can't tell us whether that was a pause or a gap in its records.
