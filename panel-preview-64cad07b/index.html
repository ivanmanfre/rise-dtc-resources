Hook
We turn down most of the dropshippers who come to us. 🙅‍♂️
The copy
We turn down most of the dropshippers who come to us. 🙅‍♂️ Their business is usually fine. The problem is margins, and our whole model runs on them. Most agencies don't care about your margins at all. For 25+ years the model has been a base fee plus a percentage of ad spend plus setup and creative fees, and whether your revenue actually moves that month or not, the agency still gets paid on the 1st. We flipped it at RISE DTC. We only get paid when we actually grow you, and we take a cut of the incremental growth, nothing off the top.
