The CPC ceiling assumes your entered conversion rate holds. Future purchases, tax, fixed overhead and production fees are excluded from this calculation.

A positive ceiling alone does not justify a launch. Confirm measurement, readiness and an approved test budget.

How the math and returns treatment work

First-order contribution before acquisition = net sales − product cost − fulfillment − payment fees − additional return costs.

Working acquisition ceiling = contribution before acquisition − contribution you want to keep. Maximum CPC = acquisition ceiling × new-customer conversion rate ÷ 100. Contribution and acquisition allowance are rounded to two decimal places; money results display two decimal places.

For example, return shipping of USD 8 on 10% of orders means USD 0.80 per original order. Enter that expected amount only if it is absent from your other costs. Reduce net sales for expected refunds once. Estimate immature cohorts carefully, then replace assumptions with matured results.

A negative allowance means the contribution target cannot be met even with free acquisition. The spend ceiling displays zero and the shortfall remains visible.
